title: "An approval compares what it stood on in three parallel ways, each with its own pin, reading, comparison and causes, while what happens next is one vocabulary"
---
An approval compares what it stood on in three ways, side by side, and each way has its own pin, its own reading of now, its own comparison and its own causes. The document itself: the approval pins its hash (seen, of DecisionVerb::Approve), freshness reads the document from the shelf as valid or as a DocFailure, valid_causes compares the two, and the causes are SelfChanged and SelfNotValid. An upstream document: the approval pins a FramePin (the link, the upstream's identity and key, its hash and an address to read it back by), freshness reads the upstream from the shelf and its Standing, pin_causes, standing_cause and link_change compare them, and the causes are UpstreamChanged, UpstreamNotValid, UpstreamDeclared, Via and LinksChanged. An artifact: the approval pins an ArtifactPin (the link, the space and the name, a PinnedArtifact that is there with a hash or not there, and the Comparison the hash was made with), read through the moves declared since as a ReadPin; freshness reads the ArtifactState the build put in the document from what the adapter of its space read, artifact_causes compares them, and the causes are ArtifactChanged, ArtifactGone, ArtifactArrived, ArtifactComparisonChanged, ArtifactMoved and ArtifactLinksChanged. Each way has its own notice for an edit declared to keep the meaning (SelfSameMeaning, UpstreamSameMeaning, ArtifactSameMeaning), asked of the ledger by its own function (doc_same_meaning for the two kinds of document, pin_same_meaning for an artifact).

What follows a cause is one vocabulary for the three. Cause::located is the one match that gives each cause an Origin (a document, an artifact, the declaration or a cycle) and an OriginEvent, so an edit of a document and an edit of an artifact are both Edited; Cause::severity is the one table of strengths; and settlements reads one row for each OriginEvent, whatever kind of thing the origin is. A new kind of thing an approval stands on would bring a pin, a reading, a comparison and causes of its own, and would reach the event, the strength and the settlement only through those two matches and that table.
