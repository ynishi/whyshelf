A common flow fixes the QA side first and writes the specification fair at release, but the sample's declaration blocks it: a verification must check at least one decision, so it cannot be written first, and a requirement no decision serves fails check, so every run fails until release; the person had to rewrite the declaration by hand. Small scaffolding tools such as cargo new and mix new offer a few built-in variants by a flag or a short name, listed in their help.
