The batch added crates/whyshelf-infra/**/*.rs to the include of the cover of cases (strength error, parts taken whole through tests, no exclude). The crate has 53 .rs files, all under src. A text search counts about 99 #[tokio::test] among those 238, leaving out rust_test.rs, whose text holds the attribute inside its fixtures; the number for rust_test.rs was not measured. Since rust-test cannot list a file with #[tokio::test] whole, these 377 tests stay pinned whole, with no rule and no code changed: an edit of any one of them makes stale every verification that names its file. Three parts hold no test and no verification names them, shelf/init.rs, shelf/mod.rs and shelf/state/mod.rs, each a part for the derive of thiserror's Error; they await an exemption by a person. The other 24 files hold no test and are no part, and need nothing.
