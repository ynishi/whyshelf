title: "A file left in a merge conflict whose sides show two identities was read as one that may hold any document, so other documents were told unknown and those of its sides were not found"
---
Since #166 a file left with both sides of a merge conflict whose sides show different identities was read as a file whose identity cannot be read, which may hold any document. It is known, though, to hold the document of one of the identities its sides show, and the shelf had no value that said so. While such a file was in the tree, a document no file carries and neither side shows was told as one that cannot be told, naming the file, and not as gone; a document of a side with no record in the ledger was not found by its identifier; a record about one that names no book was read with the rule of the whole shelf and not with the rule of the book of the file; and another file that carries the identifier of a side was valid, and not told as one that may carry it twice.

Issue #217 resolved it (DEC-115).
