title: "stale lists the approvals that no longer hold one by one, and says nothing of which of them can be mended and approved again apart from the others"
---
stale lists the approvals that no longer hold, the strongest first, with where each starts to fail (its origins). When an edit leaves many of them stale at once, a person who would hand the work to several people or agents cannot tell from it which documents can be mended and approved again apart. Two that stand on artifacts living in one file (two tests of one file have two names), one that stands on another or whose link names another, and two that wait on the approval of one upstream that is not among them (a pending upstream, or one that awaits the approvals of other names) are work on one thing: given to two hands, they meet in that file or wait on each other. Nothing in the answer says which of them share something, and the name of an artifact does not say the file it lives in.

Issue #113 resolved it with a command of its own, units, which divides what does not hold into units of work that share nothing (REQ-44, DEC-107).
