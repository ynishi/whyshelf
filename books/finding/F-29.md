When the code of crates/whyshelf-infra/src/shelf/git.rs was split out into artifact/file.rs, the tests links of V-3, V-6 and V-25 were edited by hand to the new path and approved again, and nothing in the record said the two names were one thing: a reader sees only that one name was pinned and then another. A file that only moves fares worse: every approval that pins it breaks as artifact_gone, though its content is the same, and nothing could say it had moved.
