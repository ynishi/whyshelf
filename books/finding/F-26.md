whyshelf stats on this repository named crates/whyshelf-cli/tests/command_line.rs as the origin of 61 approvals made again, more than any other artifact. It held the end-to-end tests of every command, and eleven verifications pinned it whole, so a test added for one command made the verifications of all the others stale, and they were approved again unread. When the e2e test of stats was put in its own file, that change made 3 approvals stale instead of 14.
