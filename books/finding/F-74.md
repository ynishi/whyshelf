title: "The batch over the core domain named its 873 tests by function under cases across 56 verifications, five files no verification named found a home, three parts with tests were named whole, and twenty parts with no test await the person's exemption"
---
The batch added crates/whyshelf-core/src/domain/**/*.rs to the include of the cover of cases (strength error, parts taken whole through tests, no exclude) and moved the verifications that named a file of that region whole under tests. Measured on the branch of the batch: rust-test listed 873 tests of the region in 70 files it lists whole, and 31 parts, all read: none unread. Nine parts hold tests (105 in all): six were named whole by some verification already and are reached (actor.rs, canon.rs, filter.rs, origin_tests.rs and upstream_tests.rs of freshness, untold.rs); three were named by none (location.rs, artifact.rs, artifact/listing.rs), a part only for a derive of Error or the macro opaque!, and are now named whole by the verification whose text their tests show, which gained sentences saying what they assert. Twenty-two parts hold no test: two are reached through the verification that named them, and twenty are a gap until the person exempts them, eighteen a part only for a derive of thiserror's Error and two also for a macro of their own file (tags!, name_type!) that writes a type and its methods and no test. Of the parts' reasons, 26 are a crate derive, 3 an item macro and 3 a derive beside a glob use. Before the batch two files of the region with tests were named by no verification (hash.rs with 5 tests, placement.rs with 1); each went to one verification and gained a sentence. 59 verifications named files of the region; 56 changed, and the three that named only parts did not. The tests were read by sub-area, freshness 111, ledger 200, build 166, schema 114 and the other modules 282, each test against the texts of the verifications that named its file. The 873 tests are 903 names under cases: 30 tests evidence a sentence in each of two verifications, 13 of them the tests of the glob of a book's range, which one text claims as still taking and refusing what it did and another as the rules of the range. No test fits the text of no verification, so none is a candidate for an exemption. Each test went where a sentence it is evidence of is, not only to a verification that had named its file: the 13 tests of documents' exemptions in exemption/tests.rs went to the verification of exemptions, which had not named that file, and its claims of the cover of a link and of the refusals of acceptance, shown by no test it named before, are shown by them now. Forty-one verifications changed their text: sentences were added saying what a test asserts that the text did not, and eight sentences were set right where a test asserts otherwise (a key given that does not fit the grammar, a revocation of a revocation or of a conflicted identity, an edit upstream approved again, a strength alone on a link to artifacts, the keys a cycle keeps, the order of the problems of a drop, empty readers of a document with no approval, and a document gone in the table of drift). A blind review then narrowed 12 places of sentences, added or old, to what their tests assert (among them the errors of a path, the completeness of a listing not asked, and a key given as an entry), added 4 sentences a named test asserts that no text said (a denial of a document not valid, a run judging a row as the shelf does, a sound declaration, and the problems of expansion and of the books told together), gave a verification two tests of a record's address it had lost by the move, and took off one name that evidenced no sentence of its verification; its finding that three parts (origin_tests.rs, upstream_tests.rs, untold.rs) can be listed whole was not taken, since the cover answers each as a part for a derive of Debug beside a glob use. Sentences that had no evidence before the batch are listed, not set right: among them a reading of artifact exemptions as needed or cannot tell that the file it names shows only as unneeded, an optional empty value being valid, a claim of unchanged tests, and one whose evidence is a doc test of a file the verification does not name. One sentence, that no one but SnapshotItem::compared makes what an item came to, has as its only evidence a doc test of snapshot.rs, which rust-test does not list and the cover cannot reach; snapshot.rs is split by function and cannot stay named whole, so the sentence is left as written and no name its verification holds shows it. After the batch check lists only the twenty parts that await an exemption, with the two exemptions of the trial batch, fmt --check is clean, stale lists exactly the 56 verifications changed, each with links_changed and the forty-one whose text changed also self_changed, and the test of this repository's verifications passes. The guard's tests took from 18.31 s to 25.95 s on two threads over the runs of the batch, 18.99 s on the last (11.44 s after the batch before); its candidates built in 36.05 s; the asks of a listing it made were not counted. The full fmt check, clippy, docs and tests took 5 min 56.59 s from a cold build, over five minutes: the build took most of it, the slowest test binaries being the unit tests of whyshelf-infra (22.10 s), the guard (18.10 s) and unlinked (17.30 s). The number of sentences added, and the time the split took, were not measured.
