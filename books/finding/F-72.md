title: "The trial batch over the application of whyshelf-core named its 336 tests by function under cases, 151 moved file by file and 185 split by hand from the texts of the verifications, and left two parts where no test is found for an exemption"
---
The trial batch declared the cover of cases over crates/whyshelf-core/src/app/**/*.rs (strength error, parts taken whole through tests, no exclude) and moved the verifications that named a file of that region whole under tests. Measured on the branch of the batch: rust-test listed 336 tests of files it lists whole and 4 parts. Every part is one for a derive of a crate (thiserror's Error, written thiserror::Error in dispatch.rs and fake_tests.rs and Error in error.rs and ports.rs), and every one was read: no part was unread. One part holds tests (dispatch.rs, 9 found) and three hold none (fake_tests.rs, error.rs, ports.rs). dispatch.rs, named whole by five verifications, and fake_tests.rs, named whole by two, stay named whole under tests and are reached; error.rs and ports.rs, which no verification named, are left as gaps of the cover for a person to exempt with a reason or to name. Before the batch every file of the region that holds a test was named whole by some verification, so no test of the region was named by none. 52 verifications named files of the region whole; 51 of them changed and one, which named only a part, did not; one more verification, which named no file of the region and whose text claims one of its tests, gained it, so 52 changed in all. Two of them now name nothing under tests. Of the 37 files of the region named whole, 20 were named by one verification and their 151 tests went to it, but for one test whose subject is the text of another verification; 15 were named by two to seven verifications and their 185 tests were split by hand, by reading the text of each verification that named the file and, where that did not settle it, the test itself and the sections of its file; 2 are parts. The first split left 2 tests (in the tests of the application, a failing port and the count of a sound shelf) that fit the text of no verification named by all seven that named their file, and a blind review found more tests whose verification's text did not say what they check. After it each such test has one verification, and six verifications gained a sentence saying what a test asserts: a port that fails is an error of the run, check of a sound shelf counts each book's documents, entries a book refuses, a title for a book with no title field and an entry given twice are refused, two writes at one path are refused before anything is written, and an exemption takes the name of a part only from a listing read whole with its parts; the text of one verification that said a new document outside the range asks for no identity was set right, since its test asserts one identity is taken. The 336 tests are 349 names under cases: 13 tests fit the text of two verifications and are named by both. 20 tests are still hesitated over: each went to the verification whose subject is theirs though its text does not state what they check; none fits the subject of no verification. One test's name says a retirement is recorded and it checks only a denial. Two verifications keep no test of a shared file they named whole, since their texts claim what the tests of another file show (the run of writes for new --from, and the run of the ledger). After the batch check fails only on the two parts, stale lists exactly the 52 verifications changed, each with links_changed on cases, the 51 that named the region also on tests, and the six whose text changed also self_changed, and the test of this repository's verifications passes. The guard's run over this repository took 5.44 s alone and 6.57 s to 7.75 s with its other rules on two threads; its candidates took 5.13 s; the asks of a listing it made were not counted, so they cannot be set beside the estimate of one ask each for about 70 files at 10 to 30 ms. The time the split by hand took was not measured.
