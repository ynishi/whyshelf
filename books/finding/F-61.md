title: "Whether a name or a document can be told now is split out again by each reader, and one comparison holds an arm that is never reached"
---
A name of an artifact that cannot be told now is split out again by each of seven readers (freshness, the frame of an approval, a move, same meaning, the absent and unknown links of a document, the diff of an approval and the snapshot), and what an approval saw of an artifact is held in a type that can hold "cannot tell", so the comparison of a pin with now holds an arm that is never reached and the pin side converts back to the three values. On the document side, a snapshot reads a document that cannot be told as one that is gone, which is what DEC-12 rejects; a retirement makes the tag of a document that is not valid by a match of its own, beside DocFailure::tag; and which files carry an identity now is answered by three matches of their own. Why a document that cannot be told is broken, and not a notice as an artifact that cannot be told is, is written nowhere. The other values that say a document cannot be told (its failure, its presence, the name it is read as, a lookup's absence) each answer a question of their own and keep their own arm for it; they are left as they are.
