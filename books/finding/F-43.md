title: "A required field written empty or blank was a valid value, so new wrote a document with no title and check passed it"
---
In a book that requires title, new wrote documents whose title was empty, white space only, or started with a line break, and check called them valid. A required list written as [] was valid too. required was read as the entry being there; nothing in the model said that a required field has to say something, and a tool that writes documents from other text wrote such documents without being told.
