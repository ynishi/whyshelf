title: "Units do not join the documents that wait on one upstream a new approval may not stand on, or on one document their links name and cannot be followed to, nor those whose links write one key no document has"
---
units divides what does not hold into units of work that share nothing (REQ-44). Two documents divided that stand on one upstream decided against (denied, deprecated, retired) are refused by every run of approvals until that upstream is revisited or its successor approved, and two whose links name one document that cannot be followed to (one that is not valid, a key two files write) wait until its file is made valid or a key is taken again. That mending is one piece of work for both, yet units gives them as two units: the chain of upstreams outside the division stops at such an upstream and joins nothing, and a unit says it waits on an upstream only when the upstream is pending or awaits other approvals. Two documents whose links write one key no document has are two units too, though making that document is shared work, and two made apart would write one key twice.

Issue #176 resolved it: such an upstream, and such a key, join the documents that name them, are followed no further, and a unit says for each upstream it waits on why (DEC-107).
