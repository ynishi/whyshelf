title: "A field taken out of a book's declaration left every document that held it with an entry no command found beforehand or took out afterwards"
---
Taking a field, a link or a link to artifacts out of a book's declaration makes the build tell an unknown entry for each document that wrote it, and a document that tells one is not valid. Before the declaration was edited, no command asked for the documents that hold a value for a field: status --where needs the value, and --without asks for the opposite. After it, the one form fmt writes keeps an entry its book does not declare, as the promise that a rewrite does not change what check says, so the only way to take the entry out was to open each file. The two halves of retiring a field had no command. Dropping every undeclared entry in fmt itself would have removed, without a word, the value of a mistyped name.
