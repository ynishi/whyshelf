While a verification named the files of its tests whole, a sentence of it that no test shows was not told apart from the rest. Reading each test against the text, the batches found such sentences. The blind reviews of two batches read these verifications as holding a claim that no test they named showed before the batch: V-28, V-35, V-36, V-59, V-61, V-74, V-78, V-92, V-100, V-113, V-116, V-128 and V-131 in the batch of the end-to-end tests, and V-33, V-56, V-81, V-115, V-119, V-126 and V-134 in that of the core domain. These lists are not recorded in F-73 or F-74, and which of them were given a test or a sentence set right, and which are left, is not recorded by key. F-73 states three sentences still shown by no test their verification names; F-74 lists sentences that had no evidence before the batch and says they are listed, not set right. Sentences that a test asserts otherwise were set right where the batch changed the verification: 1, 3, 8, 0, 0 and 3 in the six batches (F-72 to F-77), 15 in all. Three sentences that the tests of the integration tests of the core support only in part were listed and not changed (F-76); four sentences of verifications the batch of the infrastructure did not change read otherwise than tests of their parts and were left for a decision, with one more to look at (F-77). One sentence of V-126 says that no one but SnapshotItem::compared makes what an item came to, and gives as its evidence a doc test that does not build (books/verification/V-126.md); rust-test lists no doc test and the cover cannot reach one, so no name V-126 holds shows it (F-74), and F-74 records another sentence whose evidence is a doc test of a file its verification does not name. How many doc tests the crates hold, and how many sentences rest on one, was not measured.
