---
A built cargo test --workspace on two threads took 1 min 38.51 s in the batch of the cli sources (F-75) and 1 min 39.02 s in that of the infrastructure (F-77). The checks before a push took 1 min 56 s and 3 min 3 s warm, and 11 min 33 s and 12 min 42 s cold, in two batches, as measured by the maintainer. From a cold build the full fmt check, clippy, docs and tests took 4 min 53.79 s in the batch of the end-to-end tests (F-73), 5 min 56.59 s in that of the core domain, the build most of it (F-74), and 18 min 25.72 s in that of the cli sources, the builds 15 min 40 s of it (F-75). Between the batch of the core domain and that of the cli sources the dependencies came to be built at opt-level 2 in the dev profile, which brought a warm cargo test --workspace from 131 s to 83 s (96fd50b). The slowest binary moved with the load of the machine: unlinked.rs took 17.30 s in one batch (F-74) and 81.40 s in another, after which the load average of the machine over fifteen minutes was about 28 soon after; whether that was the load or the code was not measured (F-76). The wall time of a cold run on a machine at rest was not measured.
