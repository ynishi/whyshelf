Added up from the findings of the batches, the 40 parts that hold tests (F-80) hold 525: 9 in dispatch.rs of the application (F-72), 17 in verification_spread.rs of the end-to-end tests (F-73), 105 in nine parts of the core domain (F-74), 7 in four parts of the cli sources (F-75), 10 in two parts of the integration tests of the core (F-76) and 377 in 23 parts of the infrastructure (F-77); F-87 gives the parts of the infrastructure by their reason. No test of a part is named under cases (DEC-112): each part is named whole under tests, by one verification or more (dispatch.rs by five, F-72; architecture.rs by five, F-76; output.rs by four, F-75). So an edit of any one of these tests, or of anything else in its file, makes stale every verification that names the file, as every test did before #120. The tests of parts are 525 of the 2110 tests the findings count, the 1585 listed whole (F-78) and these; doc tests are not among them. How often such an edit has happened since, and how many approvals it made again, was not measured.
