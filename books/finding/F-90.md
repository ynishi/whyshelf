As read from the CI runs, those of #195 to #199 took up to 4 min 56 s, and from #200, which builds every dependency at opt-level 2 in the dev profile (96fd50b), they took 8 min 14 s (#200), 8 min 30 s (#201), 10 min 33 s (#202), 8 min 23 s (#203) and 10 min 36 s (#204), each over five minutes. In the CI run of #200 (run 45578734328, its log read by the maintainer), the job's cache (Swatinem/rust-cache) restored the key v0-rust-check-Linux-x64-db011040-131180e3 as a full match; the lockfiles it lists as considered hold Cargo.lock, the manifests of the members and rust-toolchain.toml, and not the root Cargo.toml, where the profile changed; the dependencies were built again, the build taking about 5 min 36 s and the tests 2 min 14 s of that run; and the cache was not saved again. Locally, a warm cargo test --workspace is faster than before (83 s against 131 s, 96fd50b). Which part of each later CI run is the build and which the tests was not measured.
