title: "Why an approval was given could be read only from the ledger's files"
---
The ledger keeps who made each decision, when and why, and no answer showed it: get, status and stale gave only the declared state, and get --approved the actor and the time. Reviewing the books of this repository meant opening the JSON files of the ledger to find why an approval stood.
