---
In each batch each test listed whole was read against the texts of the verifications that named its file; where it fit none, a sentence saying what it asserts was added, or it went to the verification whose text it shows. No test named by function is left fitting the text of no verification, and none was exempted for it (F-72 to F-77). Tests still hesitated over, each gone to the verification whose subject is nearest though its text does not state what they check, are stated as 20 in the application (F-72) and 10 in the end-to-end tests (F-73), and not for the others. A blind review took off two names that evidenced no sentence of their verification, one in the end-to-end tests (F-73) and one in the core domain (F-74). The tests of a part stay reached through the part named whole, with no sentence asked of each. Of the 36 parts with tests that were named before their batch (F-80), the tests of 25 were read against the bodies of the verifications that name them: the two of the integration tests of the core, architecture.rs and split_modules.rs, 10 tests of which 3 of split_modules.rs evidence no sentence and check what no decision says, what a split of a module keeps and what a doc comment may name (F-76); and the 23 of the infrastructure, 377 tests of which 58 evidence no sentence (F-77). The 61 are recorded and not given sentences. The tests of the other 11 are not stated as read that way: dispatch.rs of the application (F-72), verification_spread.rs of the end-to-end tests (F-73), six parts of the core domain (F-74) and three of the cli sources (F-75); how many of them evidence no sentence is not known. Sentences added are counted in some findings only: six verifications gained a sentence (F-72), eleven sentences were added (F-73), sixteen places in fifteen verifications (F-75) and one sentence (F-76); F-74 says its number was not measured and F-77 does not count it. No total is given.
