title: "What a change made stale was approved again by a script of the repository, with the order of its books written in"
---
After each change in whyshelf's own work, the approvals it made stale were approved again in one run, upstream first, by a script that read stale's JSON, sorted the keys by an order of books written in the script, and passed them to one judge approve. It was used for every change shipped in a day, from 5 to 22 approvals at a time, and only knew this repository's books; nothing kept it from approving a broken approval again.
