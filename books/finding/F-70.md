title: "Where one end of a change alone cannot tell a name, the two holdings of a document differ by the reading, so a difference of holdings does not show that the change reached the document"
---
A gate on whether a change stays inside the documents it was meant for needs to know which documents the change reaches. The answer first to hand is impact's own: the two holdings of a document differ (changed). Where one end alone cannot tell a name an approval stands on, that difference is one of the reading, not of the change: the docs were not built in the directory of the end before and were built in the one after, so one end cannot tell and the other holds, though the change touched nothing. A holding not there does not say that the document has no file on the shelf either: the standings an assessment lists hold the subjects of the ledger too, so a document with a record and no file is listed. Evidence that a change reaches a document is then of two kinds only: answers that read no pin (what was decided, Ledger::status_of; whether it is on the shelf, Shelf::state_of; its content, Doc::hash; what its links name, Doc::links_named; the records about it, Record::about), and the answer that tells a name it cannot tell apart by its own rule (what the change made, of impact).

Issue #174 resolved it: the gate reads only those answers (DEC-110).
