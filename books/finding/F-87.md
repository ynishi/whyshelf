Of the 525 tests pinned whole (F-81), 17 of verification_spread.rs in the end-to-end tests, a part only for #[tokio::test] (F-73), and 139 of 11 parts of the infrastructure whose reason cover gives is #[tokio::test] (F-77) are in files whose reason is that macro of a crate, which may write more than one test (DEC-111). The other 238 tests of the infrastructure are in 12 parts whose reason is the derive of thiserror's Error, about 99 of them also under #[tokio::test] by a text search (F-77). Rewriting the tests as #[test] over a shared runtime would let rust-test list a file whole, and its tests be named by function, where #[tokio::test] is its only reason; it is so for verification_spread.rs (F-73), and whether it is so for each of the 11 parts of the infrastructure was not measured. The 238 would stay parts for the derive. How many of these tests need an async runtime, and what the rewrite would cost, was not measured. The batch of the infrastructure was run last and named its parts whole as they were: all 23 parts with tests were named before it, and none needed a new name (F-77).
