title: "A document meant to stay unreached by a covering link could only be left listed, or the rule weakened"
---
A link declared with cover expects every document of its target book to be reached; a document kept on purpose that no link reaches, such as a finding that grounds no requirement, stayed listed forever under a warn cover, and under a true cover the only way out was to weaken the rule for the whole book. Linters keep such exceptions at the item with a reason, and Rust's expect tells when one is no longer met.
