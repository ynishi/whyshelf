Since #182 stale offers an approval of one document by name only where the frame would take it. Where the frame refuses it the command is left out, and nothing took its place. In most cases the refusal is told elsewhere in the answer, but in two nothing said why an origin has no approving command: a pending upstream whose own depends link names a denied document or an artifact that cannot be told now, which no approval pinned, so no origin and no notice tells the refusal; and a pending upstream whose approval would close a cycle among the frames of approvals. The reason was at hand: what is foreseen keeps the answer of the frame for each document asked about, but the one way to read it answered yes or no, and the settlements a no dropped were dropped with their reason.

Issue #184 resolved it: an approval the frame refuses is told with the refusal (DEC-114).
