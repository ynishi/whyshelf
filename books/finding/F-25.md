title: "How the work went was counted by hand after every change"
---
Using whyshelf on its own books, how many approvals a change made stale, how often a decision moved, how long a document stayed pending and which files kept being the origin of staleness were counted by hand after each change. The ledger already held what these need: every record's time and actor, identities that carry the time they were made, and the pins of every approval.
