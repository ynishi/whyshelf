title: "Reading the side before a change one way (Git's listing) and the side after another (the file system), then holding the two against each other, leaves a gap wherever the two implementations differ"
---
An answer of whether a change makes an approval not hold was first designed by reading the side before from what Git lists of a commit and the side after from the file system, and holding the paths of the one against the readings of the other where they meet. Each review found another case where the two disagree: a symbolic link, a submodule, the case of a name on a file system that does not tell case apart, Git's conversions of line endings and the like, a file an ignore rule names, a pipe. Closing each case left the two ways of reading in place, so a new case appeared with each look. The cause is that the two readings were not behind one port and did not go through the one way every other reading of the project goes; a difference between two implementations is a difference in the answer.
