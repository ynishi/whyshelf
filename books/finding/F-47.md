title: "Writing nothing had four forms with three meanings: a field left out had no value, a null made the document invalid, an empty or blank text was a value, and a blank list item passed"
---
An optional field written source: "" was valid and read as a value, which a filter on an empty value and status saw, while the same field written source: (a YAML null) made the document invalid; a list item null was a problem and a list item "" was not; new dropped empty items and check let them pass. A text could hold a line break wherever it was declared, a title included, and nothing in a declaration could say a text was one line. Required fields had been settled by #123; the rest had no meaning in the model.
