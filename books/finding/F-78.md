title: "Over the six batches 1585 tests that rust-test lists whole were named by function under cases, and their split between the verifications that named a file was read by hand"
---
Issue #120 moved the verifications of this repository, region by region, from naming the files of their tests whole under tests to naming the tests by function under cases (F-72 to F-77), and a last change took every Rust file of the crates into the include (def460d). Added up from the six findings, rust-test listed whole 1585 tests in the files of the regions: 336 in the application of the core (F-72), 251 in the end-to-end tests (F-73), 873 in the core domain (F-74), 96 in the cli sources (F-75), 2 in the integration tests of the core (F-76) and 27 in the infrastructure (F-77). They are at least 1607 names under cases: 349, 255, 903 and 98 (F-72 to F-75) and the 2 of F-76; F-77 does not state its number of names. A test is named by two verifications where it evidences a sentence of each: 13, 4, 30 and 2 tests (F-72 to F-75); F-76 and F-77 state none. Where one verification named a file, its tests went to it: 151 tests in 20 files of the application, but for one test whose subject is the text of another verification (F-72), 152 in 40 files of the end-to-end tests (F-73), the 2 of the integration tests of the core (F-76) and the 27 of three files of the infrastructure (F-77). Where several named a file, each test was read against the text of each and against its own assertions: 185 tests in 15 files of the application (F-72) and 99 in 12 of the end-to-end tests (F-73); F-74 and F-75 do not give their split in these terms. F-72 to F-75 say that the time the split took was not measured and that the asks of a listing were not counted; F-76 and F-77 say nothing of either. The guard's candidates took 5.13 s (F-72), 7.42 s (F-73), 8.62 s (F-76) and 4.91 s (F-77) to run, and were built in 36.05 s (F-74) and, from a cold build, in 5 min 54 s (F-75), 3 min 26 s (F-76) and 2 min 37 s (F-77). No finding records a name that came to a gap for a spelling other than the one the source writes; such gaps were not counted.
