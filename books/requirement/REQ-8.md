title: "Every fault of the documents and the ledger is reported; none is dropped and none stops the rest"
---
Invalid files, cycles, records that contradict one another and ledger files that are no record are carried in every answer that reads the documents, and everything else is still answered. A reader learns what is wrong without a second command, and a fault in one document never hides the others. A declaration of books that cannot be used is itself the answer: nothing can be read through it.
