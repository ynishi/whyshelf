title: "What a change made stale is approved again in one step, upstream first, and nothing broken is approved in silence"
---
A person who has looked again at what a change made stale approves it all in one run, upstream before what stands on it, with one reason, and with no order of books written anywhere. An approval that is broken is never approved again by that step: it is named, so that it is mended first.
