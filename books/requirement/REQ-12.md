title: "A hole in the chain of reasons is reported"
---
A project says which documents are expected to have others standing on them, such as a requirement a decision serves and a decision a verification checks. A document no such link reaches is reported without a search by hand, so that a requirement nothing meets or a decision nothing checks is seen as soon as it exists.
