title: "What a measurement expects can be shown to have been there before its result"
---
A person can keep the expectation of a measurement and its result as two documents, approve the expectation before the run, so the ledger's time shows its approval came before the result's and any later edit of it makes it stale, and approve the result after the run on top of it.
