title: "An artifact that moved is declared once, and the approvals on its old name are read through it"
---
A person can declare, with a reason, that an artifact moved from one name to another, and from then on an approval that pinned the old name is judged on the new one: a move alone leaves it as it was, a move with an edit names the edit, and a link still writing the old name is told; the record keeps who said so, when and why.
