title: "An answer about a document shows who decided about it, when and why"
---
Every row of an answer, the form in which an answer shows a document with its standing, shows the decision that stands about it, as its record keeps it: who made it, when, and the reason given. A person learns why an approval stands without reading the ledger's files.
