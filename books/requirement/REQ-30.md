title: "The documents of some books, standings or field values can be asked for, by a rule of the model"
---
A person asks status for the documents of chosen books, of chosen standings, whose declared field holds a given value, whose declared field holds a value whichever it is, or whose declared field holds none, and the answer has only the documents that pass, with the filter as given. Which documents pass is a rule of the model, written once; a filter that names a book or a field the declaration does not have, a value no declaring book can read, or a question no document could pass for asking a field to have a value and none, or none of a field every declaring book requires, is refused as a request, with every reason.
