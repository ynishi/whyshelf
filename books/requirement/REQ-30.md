title: "The documents of some books, standings or field values can be asked for, by a rule of the model"
---
A person asks status for the documents of chosen books, of chosen standings, or whose declared field holds a value, and the answer has only the documents that pass, with the filter as given. Which documents pass is a rule of the model, written once; a filter that names a book or a field the declaration does not have, or a value no declaring book can read, is refused as a request, with every reason.
