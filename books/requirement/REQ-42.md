title: "Given documents, the documents that depend on them and what all of those stand on can be asked, with the files of the project it is in"
---
A person or an agent names one document or more and is answered the documents that depend on them, through their Depends links and transitively, and the artifacts all of those stand on, by space: what their links to artifacts name now and what the approvals that stand about them pinned. Each artifact says where it lives in the files of the project, whole or in part, or that its space cannot say and why, and the answer gives the files of the project all of it is in. What may depend on the named and cannot be read (a document whose links cannot be read, a file whose identity cannot be read) is said, counted, so that an answer is never taken for the whole when it may not be. Asking writes nothing.
