title: "Where approvals stop holding, the answer says what can be said or done about it, and does none of it"
---
Each origin of the approvals that no longer hold comes with the commands that can settle it, as they are typed and in the order a person would try them: reading the change first, then the declarations that apply to what happened there, then approving. A command is offered only where it can settle that origin. The tool says none of it: what an edit means is still a person's to say.
