title: "Who read the content an approval stands on is answered for every approved document, in every project, and the approvals read by one name can be asked for"
---
For every approved document, an answer says which names have read the content its approval stands on now, and which readings of it have lapsed, whether or not a rule asks for more than one name. A person can ask status for the approvals read by nobody now, by one name, or by several, so the approvals no second actor has read are listed in any project. Reading this changes no judgement: who may decide, what an approval repeats and the count a rule asks for stay as they are.
