title: "Documents never approved are approved in one step, upstream first, as far as a person asks"
---
A person can approve, with one reason, every document that has never been approved, or those a document stands on, in an order where each upstream is decided first, so that bringing documents into books ends with nothing stale; nothing approved or broken is approved by it in silence.
