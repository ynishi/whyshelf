title: "One document can be exempted from a cover with a reason, and an exemption no longer needed is told"
---
A person can record that one document is not expected to be reached by one covering link, with a reason, so the rule stays for the rest of the book; the record keeps who, when and why, an exemption that adds nothing is refused, and one that is no longer needed, or held twice, is told until it is withdrawn.
