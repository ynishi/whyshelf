title: "A link to artifacts can expect every name of a selection to be reached by a document, and a gap fails or warns as a cover into a book does"
---
A link to artifacts may declare a cover over a selection of the names of its space, for files the include and exclude patterns a book's range uses. Every name selected that is there now is expected to be reached by that link from a pending or approved document. A gap of an error cover fails check and names the name; a warn cover lists it and leaves check clean. One name can be exempted from one covering link, with a reason, by a person, and the exemption is taken back by withdrawing it. When the names cannot all be listed, an error cover does not pass: a gate that cannot see the whole set does not say it holds.
