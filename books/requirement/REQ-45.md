title: "Whether a change stays inside the reach of named documents, as the shelf stood before it, can be asked of every document, and a check can fail on it"
---
A person or an agent names the documents a change is meant for. For every registered document the answer says whether the change reaches it (it adds a cause that makes its approval not hold, takes such a cause away, changes what was decided about it, puts it on the shelf or takes it off, changes its content or what its links name, or adds, withdraws or rewrites a record about it), and if it does, whether it is inside the reach of the named documents as the shelf stood before the change: the named documents and those that depend on them. A document the change reaches outside that reach is told apart from one that cannot be shown outside it (the change adds it or puts it back, or the shelf before has files whose links cannot be read), and from one of which whether the change reaches it cannot be told, with what cannot be told and in which direction. What cannot be read at all at either end, a file of the books whose identity cannot be read or a file of the ledger that is no record, is answered too, and the gate never passes over it. Asked to, the command exits 1 unless the gate passes, so a check of changes worked on in parallel can fail on one that reaches into the documents of another. Asking writes nothing.
