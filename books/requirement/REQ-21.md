title: "How the work goes is derived from the ledger, the same each time it is asked"
---
A person can ask how the work on the books has gone, how often each document was approved again, how long documents stayed pending, how large the runs of judgements were and which documents and artifacts most often stood under an approval made again, and the answer comes from the ledger and the documents alone, so the same ledger always gives the same answer.
