title: "A record keeps who ran a judgement apart from who answers for it"
---
A record names who answers for it and, when another ran the command (a coding agent running judge for a person, say), that one too, so that neither name is lost. Both names are as declared, with no authentication, as every actor is; nothing committed to the project chooses either of them. The second name is left out when the two are the same, so a record written before reads back unchanged and with the hash it had, and a record is not written in two forms. The second name changes no judgement: who may decide, who has read a document, what an approval repeats and how the work is counted read the one who answers for the record.
