title: "What does not hold can be divided into units of work that share nothing, each to be mended and approved again apart"
---
A person or an agent asks for the approvals that no longer hold, and on request the pending documents as well, divided into units of work. Two documents are in one unit when one stands on the other or a Depends link of one names the other, when they stand on artifacts that live in one file of the project, whole or in part, or when the same upstreams outside the documents divided hold both back from being approved, directly or through what holds such an upstream back in turn, which joins as the documents divided do; the units are the parts this joins. So no two units share a file their artifacts live in, a document, or an upstream they wait on, or what such an upstream waits on in turn, and each can be mended and approved again by a hand of its own. Each unit gives its documents in the order a run of approvals takes them, each with where it stands, the files its artifacts live in, its artifacts with where each lives, and the upstreams it waits on with why. An artifact whose place cannot be told is said, with why, and joins only what stands on the same name: that two units share no file is not said of it. The same shelf gives the same answer, whatever order its files are read in. Asking writes nothing.
