title: "Whether a change makes approvals not hold can be asked, reading both ends of it the same one way"
---
A person or an agent names a change by the way it is read: through Git, two commits or a commit and the index; or through two directories. Both ends are read that same one way, and for every registered document the answer says whether the change adds a cause that makes its approval not hold, by the rule stale uses: yes, with where it starts; no; or cannot tell, with what cannot be told. What an adapter or the assessment cannot tell is carried as it is, never read as holding. Asking writes nothing.
