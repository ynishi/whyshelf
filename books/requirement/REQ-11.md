title: "Every command works outside version control"
---
A project in a plain directory has the same freshness as one in Git. Version control is used, when it is there, to name and read back the content an approval saw and to keep the revision as a hint; its configured user name, read even outside a repository, says who judges when nothing else does. No freshness depends on any of it.
