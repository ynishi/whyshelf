title: "What an approval saw can be read back and compared with today"
---
For any approval, the approved document, the upstream documents and the files it stood on can be shown as they were, and set against what they are now. The verdict does not depend on it; reading back is for a person reviewing a stale approval, auditing one, or restoring a text.
