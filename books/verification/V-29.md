Over small shelves: links of every meaning, a self-link, an unresolved key and a link to artifacts give the facts in and out; a retired holder reaches nothing; an invalid file is not counted; a book linked only from itself still lists its lone document as isolated, while a book linked only by replaces and a book cut off by its declaration are left out as the rule says; a single book with one link into itself lists its documents as each fits; and no document is in two lists. In the app: the default answer, both filters, an unusable declaration answered with the lists asked for empty and the others null, and an unknown book.
