title: "Every reading of a decision about an upstream agrees on each way an upstream stands, the words tell each apart, and the ledger reads nothing that reads it"
---
Core (upstream_tests.rs): one table, a row for each way an upstream stands (no decision, approved fresh with its quorum met, short of names, short and stale, stale, broken, expired and nothing else, denied, deprecated with a successor, deprecated with none, retired), and for each row every reading of it: whether frame_approve refuses a new approval on it and which decision it names, the same as whether the ledger lets a new approval stand on it (Ledger::may_stand_on), whether it counts, which decision an exemption of it is unneeded for, what it is to what stands on it, the causes that come of it with their strength, the events at the origins, whether a run holds what stands on it back with it in the run, out of the run and left out of it, whether --stale is foreseen to approve again what stands on it when that is stale, and whether approving again settles what it makes stale. The refusal, the count and the reason of the exemption name the same decision in every row, a row that is no decision against has none, and an upstream decided against holds nothing back whatever it is, until it is left out of the run. Core (approve_stale_tests.rs): a document that stands on an upstream the frame refuses and then on one that holds back is held back by the second, as the first holds nothing back; and what a run of --stale is foreseen to approve again is what it approves, when an approval of it is refused by the frame and when one stands on such. Core (ledger/tests.rs): the ledger lets a new approval stand on an upstream with no decision, an approved one and one whose denial was revoked, and not on one denied, deprecated or retired, naming that status; what is foreseen of an approval's frame is the refusal frame_approve gives, the same value, for a document not valid, a link that cannot be followed, an upstream decided against, an artifact that cannot be told and a cycle of links, while a cycle among the frames of approvals is not foreseen and frame_approve refuses it. Command line (cause.rs, cause_tests.rs): each way an upstream does not stand as a settled approval has words of its own, no two alike, a pending one is told as having no decision that stands about it, and an exemption of a document decided against is told as decided_against whichever decision it was; the same readings on the binary are V-125. Architecture (architecture.rs, arch-lint.toml): the rule of the ledger is run as arch-lint.toml declares it over fixtures in a directory of its own, and refuses a use of a module that reads the ledger (as super::super and as super from the ledger itself), a path written in full in code, a module of the shelf that no list names and a glob import of the shelf, and leaves out a file of tests, a link of a doc comment, a module the ledger reads and its own children; what it does not see (a path inside a macro, a module through an alias, a re-export) is on the test, for a review to read. The allowed modules are in the exclude of the rule and in DEC-97, and this verification names the rule, so a change of the one makes the other stale.
