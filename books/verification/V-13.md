title: "A reference is read in one order, and several hits are listed"
---
The four shapes are tried in order; an unknown book, a refused key and a missing key are told apart; a short identity matches the end; ambiguity lists every candidate. A short identity is 8 to 32 lower-case hex digits, and 7 digits, 33, a capital or a letter past f is refused. An empty text is no reference, and a book that is no name or a key with a space, a second colon or nothing after the colon is refused as the text is read; each of the four shapes written out reads back the same; and a document whose file is gone is still found by its identity in the ledger, while its key names nothing.
