title: "The drawing of stats is its answer in five sections, exactly, the same each time"
---
A small answer gives the expected text exactly; an empty ledger says so in every section; durations read as hours and minutes, under a minute and negative included, and times as RFC 3339 UTC to the second; with very long names every line stays under 100 characters, cut with an ellipsis; a CJK actor aligns by display width; an origin outside the space file shows its space; a list drawn short gives its whole count; a time out of range is drawn as milliseconds, not as a time; the same answer gives the same text twice. A date reads as UTC midnight, Z and an offset give their instant, and a time with no offset, a date in another form and no time at all are refused.
