title: "Every cause has its strength, staleness passes through frames, and notices change nothing"
---
An approval lapses at its expiry and not before, and is then expired; the strength of each cause follows the table; a degradation takes its strongest; a cycle is broken with no Via inside; one event is one root, an edit or a cycle reached two ways up included, and an edit upstream approved again is still one root of what saw the older text, while an upstream approved again and then edited again is two roots below; an edit inside a cycle of frames is a root of the member that pinned it; informs links and expired upstreams give notices only; an informs link to a fresh approval gives no notice, and a pending or a denied document still has its notices; an upstream gone is said once, by its pin, while another key no document has is a change of links; a denied, deprecated or retired document is a standing with no freshness; and the kinds of standing are listed once, in the order pending, denied, deprecated, retired, then fresh, awaiting, expired, stale and broken. Documents are put in components upstream first: a chain comes in its order, a cycle is one component after what it stands on, a ring and a document that links to itself are each found as a cycle named by the graph it is found in, and a chain of twenty thousand documents is read with no overflow of the stack.
