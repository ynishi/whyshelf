title: "From hand-written pages, the promise of an item, a member, a variant with its fields, a field, a module and a primitive is read, and only the declaration or the docs change its text"
---
Over pages written by hand in the layout of rustdoc 1.95, with words of this repository only: an item gives its declaration and its top doc, a method the code header and the docblock inside its toggle or none, a deprecated method its docs and not the mark, a field the code and the docblock after it, a variant its docs and the fields listed under it, a module, a crate, a primitive and a keyword their docs only, and attribute macros their declaration. An item without docs promises its declaration and no docs, and a docblock past the next heading is not its own. The text puts decl before each line of the declaration and doc before each of the docs, empty lines empty, so a line of the docs that says decl is not a declaration. A longer body, another line Source, a deprecation mark, another list of impls and an impl added after the last method leave the text of every item as it was; another declaration (a public field added to a struct among them), other docs, docs written or taken away change it, and only that item's. A docblock quoted with single quotes is read, entities named and numeric are put back, and a tag whose attribute holds a greater-than sign is one tag. A blank line added to the docs leaves the hash of lines as it was and changes the hash of bytes. A kind with a declaration and a page without one, a page with no main content, a member not on its page and an element never closed are Unreadable, and the reading is there for presence with the reason while lines and bytes cannot be told; current gives the text, and none for what is absent, unreadable or not built.
