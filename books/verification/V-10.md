title: "A decision is refused before any record exists when its frame or its invariants do not hold"
---
An approval that would close a cycle, of a document that is not valid, or of an upstream decided against is refused and writes nothing; a retirement needs no file; an expiry must come after the decision. A denial or a deprecation of a document that is invalid or gone is refused as not valid, and of a valid one is framed with the hash it sees.
