title: "The sides of a conflict make one identity or one of several, and the shelf, the lookup, the book of a record, the filter, the counts, the gate and each refusal tell a document a side shows as unknown in that file and every other as without it"
---
The identity of the sides (build/tests.rs): none is no identity, one or the same twice is that one, and two different are one of them, in order; such a file is kept apart, with its sides and no identity, and no other file has sides. What an identity comes to beside it: one a side shows cannot be told and may be in that file, beside a file that may hold any too, one no side shows is gone, or, beside a file that may hold any, cannot be told in that file only; one another file carries is the invalid document of that file. The identities the shelf knows are those carried and those a side shows, and the identities of the shelf are the carried ones. The book of one a side shows is the book of the file, none when two such files or a file that may hold any are under two books, and none when only a file that may hold any may hold it. A file that carries an identity a side shows and the file of that side are both told so, once for each identity, and a file whose sides show other identities is not; the invalid files are in their order. The sentence of the failure names the files a side shows it in before those that may hold any (said_tests.rs). The lookup (lookup/tests.rs) names one a side shows by its identifier and its short identifier with no record, and with a record names the file; one no side shows is gone, or beside a file that may hold any cannot be told there. A record naming no book is read with the book of the file (admit_tests.rs); the filter by book takes one a side shows in that book and no other (filter.rs). reach counts the file while a side it shows is not reached (reach.rs), units while a side is divided or named (units/tests.rs), and the gate does not pass while it is at either end, and leaves a document no side shows gone and not claimed (within/tests.rs). rekey says one no side shows is gone and one a side shows cannot be told (rekey_tests.rs); the snapshot, a retirement, an approval, a frame of presence, an exemption and a comparison tell each as gone or not to be told (now_tests.rs); get tells one a side shows as one that cannot be told and an approval of it is refused (app/shelf/tests.rs). The reader (docs.rs): both sides of two documents, with the delimiter shared, with the marker first and with a common ancestor between them, show one of the two identities.
