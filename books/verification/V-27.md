title: "The map of a schema holds its books and links in order, and a description is one line"
---
The map of a schema keeps the declaration's order and shows a self-link, an artifact link with its comparison and ignore prefixes, cover, and every count. An empty declaration maps to no book and no link. A description is kept, and a blank one, one of invisible characters only, or one holding a line break or another control character is refused. The map reads no artifact, and an unsound declaration gives the problems check gives. The drawing of a fixed schema is an exact text, prefixes that hold a comma included, and the JSON has its books, links and drawing.
