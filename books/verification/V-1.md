title: "Approving makes fresh; editing upstream makes downstream stale, naming the upstream"
---
Approve an upstream and a downstream, edit the upstream; stale lists both, and the downstream's root is the upstream's edit. Renaming a key or moving a file within its book keeps an approval; moving it to another book does not. The standing of a document the assessment lists is its own, and of an identity it does not list is pending, with no notices. The hash of a document changes with a field, with the type a value is read as, or with the body, and not with where a link points, to another key or to no document. Documents whose links stand on each other are a cycle, even when pending, and one that stands on itself is a cycle of one; a document that only stands on a cycle is not in it, and a chain, informs and replaces links, and a ring through an invalid document make no cycle.
