title: "A row shows the decision that stands, with who, when and why"
---
A pending document has no decision; after an approval with a reason, the row names that record and its reason; a later denial is the decision then, the one the declared state names; withdrawing the denial brings the approval back, in the row and in the declared state, and withdrawing that leaves none. Over a port that fails, status is an error of the run (Failed), not an answer.
