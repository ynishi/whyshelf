---
Every kind of cause is located with its event, the upstream's status split three ways; an origin gathers its events and the approvals that hold its causes, an approval reached through an upstream being degraded but no holder. Each row of the table gives its settlements in order and once; approving again is offered only for an edit, an arrival or a changed link whose approvals the shelf's choice takes, never for a pending or superseded upstream, a link behind a move, a gone file or an expiry alone; a declaration origin has no same-meaning declaration. Each settlement is one command that parses with the real arguments, with or without a root, and a document is named by its key in its book or by its identity.
