title: "Each kind of cause has one origin and event, and an origin's settlements follow the table, written as commands that parse"
---
Every kind of cause is located with its event, the upstream's status split three ways; an origin gathers its events and the approvals that hold its causes, an approval reached through an upstream being degraded but no holder. Each row of the table gives its settlements in order and once; the diff offered is that of the smallest approval that holds the cause, not of a smaller one that only sees it through another, and where one approval of an origin holds its expiry and another its edit, it is the diff of the one that holds the edit; a superseded upstream, a link behind a move, an upstream decided against, a document not valid and a cycle of frames have no settlement at all; approving again is offered only for an edit, an arrival or a changed link whose approvals the shelf's choice takes, never for a pending or superseded upstream, a link behind a move, a gone file or an expiry alone, nor for an edit of an approval on an upstream deprecated with a successor or a link rewritten to a document denied, whose approval the frame refuses; an expiry, a pending upstream and an upstream awaiting names are offered their approving command only when the frame takes the approval of the document: not for an expired approval on an upstream deprecated with a successor or of a document not valid, a pending upstream with a link that names a document denied, a link that cannot be followed or an artifact that cannot be told now, or whose approval would close a cycle among the frames, or an awaiting upstream whose own upstream is denied, and still for each when the frame takes it; an approval by name of a document the frame was not asked about is not offered, and the documents the frame is asked about are those the settlements approve by name: the document whose approval lapsed and the pending upstream itself, and none for an edit or a gone file; a declaration origin has no same-meaning declaration. Each settlement is one command that parses with the real arguments, with or without a root, and a document is named by its key in its book or by its identity.
