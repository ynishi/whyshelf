A link to artifacts with no space or an unknown one is refused. A text that is no name, or that the adapter answers is not a name, makes the document invalid. An approval on a name that cannot be told is refused and writes nothing; a pinned name that later cannot be told is a notice and the approval stays fresh. presence ignores content and tells present from absent. A stored pin without a space reads as file and its record is unchanged, and every record of the repository's own ledger reads with no problem and writes back as stored, with the same hash. One name in two spaces is two artifacts.
