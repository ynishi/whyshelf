title: "An edit is one origin of all it degrades, a shared file included"
---
Every degraded approval is under an origin and nothing else is, in every case below; with nothing degraded there is none. An edited document is one origin of every approval below it. An upstream approved again since is the origin of what saw its older text. A file pinned by four approvals is one origin of the four; with an edited document and a gone file too, an approval under several is listed under each, and the file of four comes before the document of three. Inside a cycle of frames, an edit is still the origin of what pinned it, and the cycle is one origin of its members. An upstream denied is the origin of what stands on it, and one whose file is gone of itself and all that stand on it. A Via has no origin, an upstream change starts at the upstream and an expiry at its holder. On the binary, stale answers the origin of an edited document.
