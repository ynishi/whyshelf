---
On the binary and a small project of files where a requirement, and a record of no book, needs two different names of maintainers (ana, bo): after ana's judge approve with an --until a few seconds ahead, status shows the requirement awaiting, with awaiting readers ana and need 2; after bo's judge approve of the same content it is fresh, with no awaiting; once ana's approval lapses it is awaiting again, with bo alone as its reader, and then check exits 0 and lists it under awaiting with a note on stderr, stale --exit-code exits 0, and status --standing awaiting lists it. With both names and no expiry, check lists nothing as awaiting.
