title: "Every use case that writes records puts them on disk once at the end of its run, and a write that fails part way is told as the records written, or as not durable"
---
For judge, approve of the chosen documents, same meaning, exempt, moved, judge moved --prefix and withdraw: every record is written, then put on disk once, with the path of each; a write that fails part way is told as the records written, put on disk first, and its class is failed; a write that fails before any is its own error and puts nothing on disk; records all written and not put on disk are not durable, kept and named. No module of the use cases but the one of the run appends a record. The ledger writes a record create-only and whole, a record taken is left as it was, and a record whose write fails leaves no file.
