title: "Each word of a count, a type and a meaning reads back to its value, an unknown one is a problem naming where it was written, and the problems of a document say the declared words"
---
Every value of a count, a field type and a meaning is read back from its word, and no other word is read; a link that writes no count is many, and a link put back into words expands to the same link; an unknown count, type or meaning, the meaning of a preset included, is a problem naming the book and the entry or the preset, with the words it takes, told beside the other problems; a declaration file with such words is read, and they come back as problems; the problems of a document say the count with what it allows, the type by its word, the written value by its kind, and the book of an undeclared entry, never the Rust value.
