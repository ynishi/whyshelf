The order is upstream first on a chain and a diamond, ignores documents outside the set, counts the pins, lets the links decide a ring the links and pins close together, and holds back a document standing on a broken, pending or held-back one. Over the fake: an edited upstream and its two downstream approvals are approved upstream first; an expired one is approved again and its old expiry is not carried over; an artifact gone, an invalid upstream and a Via-only broken one are left out as broken; one newly linking a broken one, and one on a pending upstream, are left out as stands_on; one on a deprecated upstream is left out as refused while the rest is written; a pending document is not touched; an absent name is pinned and listed; --until and --actor reach every record; a past --until with nothing stale is refused and writes nothing; and nothing stale is an empty run.
