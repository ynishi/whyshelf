title: "What settles an origin lists an approval the frame takes and withholds one it refuses with why, and the answer of stale tells each withheld with its document, kind and sentence"
---
In the domain (settle.rs): a pending upstream the frame refuses is withheld with the refusal, whether its link names a document decided against, an artifact that cannot be told, or its approval would close a cycle; one the frame takes is listed and nothing is withheld; two approvals of one document that are both refused are two withheld, in the order of the list; and an approval by name is the approval of its kind, and no other settlement is one. In the answer (output/stale_tests.rs): each approval withheld is told with its document, the word of its kind and the sentence of the refusal; it is told when the root of the run is no text and no command can be written; each is one line on stderr with its kind, the same document twice two lines; and nothing withheld writes no line.
