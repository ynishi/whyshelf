---
The division (units/tests.rs): two chains that share nothing are two units, each upstream first, with the files of their artifacts, nothing waited on and nothing unplaced, each document with the kind of its standing; what is divided is the set of degraded approvals, and every origin's approvals are of it. Two chains that stand on one name, and two names that live in one file (whole and in part), are one unit. Two stale documents that the same pending upstream outside holds back are one unit that waits on it, and only one of them two units; with the pending divided too, the upstream is a document of the unit, first, and nothing waits on it, each document's kind the one its standing gives. A hold on the second target of a link and on the second link joins as well. A pending document whose link names an undone document that is not valid is one unit with it, in either order of identities, which is the order of upstream_first; one whose link names a key an undone document writes with another file is one unit with it; two whose links name one document outside the division that is not valid are one unit that waits on it, counted once. A unit joined by a name alone, where another document stands on the one that names, comes in the order of upstream_first. A name whose place cannot be told joins only the same name, and is listed with why and its documents; the names asked are the names divided, and one the answers do not hold has no place and says so. A cycle of pending links and a broken cycle are each one unit, by identity. The same shelf read in another order is divided the same. The chain (units/tests.rs): two stale documents held back by two pending upstreams, one of which stands on the other, are one unit that waits on both, and two units when it does not; with the pending divided the chain is empty and the four are one unit in the order of upstream_first, waiting on nothing. A pending upstream whose test lives in the file a divided document's test lives in joins the two, and the unit lists only its own documents of that artifact; in another file they are two units, and the chain's file and artifact are in neither. The names of the chain are asked with the rest, and with no answer a name the chain and a divided document share joins them by name, and every name with no place is listed with its documents, the chain's among them, while the unit lists only its own. A pending upstream that stands on a stale document of another unit joins the two. Two stale documents held back by the same awaiting upstream are one unit that waits on it. A chain stops at an approval that holds: two pending upstreams on one fresh document are two units, and a stale document on a fresh one whose test lives in a divided document's file is a unit apart. No order passes through the chain: a stale document that waits on a pending upstream standing on another stale one comes in the order of upstream_first over the two alone, first. What could join units unseen is counted: nothing on a clean shelf, a divided document that is not valid, one outside that a divided document's link names, one that only a document of the chain names, not one nothing divided or of the chain names, and a file whose identity cannot be read. What is followed no further (units/tests.rs): documents divided or of a chain that wait on one upstream for a decision against it, or on one document their links cannot be followed to, are one unit, the upstream is followed no further, and it is told once with its reasons for the documents divided; so are those whose links write one key no document has, which is not told; and the division is the same read in any order. So two stale documents on one upstream deprecated with a successor, on one their links were changed to that is denied, or on one they link to that is denied after their approval, are one unit that waits on it refused; one that only pinned it is apart, and one upstream two links name is told once. An upstream denied that a document of the chain and a divided document stand on joins them, and is told when the divided one stands on it; when only the chain stands on it, it joins and is not told; what it stands on is not read, nor its test asked. The files of a key two files write join the documents that link to it and are waited on, except the one divided. One identity two files carry, one of them of a collision, is told once with both reasons, whether one document or two name it, and counted once. Each upstream is one entry of one kind. A key no file writes, or only a file whose identity cannot be read or one with no id, joins the documents that link to it and is not told, while two keys are two units, and a text that is no key joins nothing; a document not valid among the divided that a link names is not waited on. The order of a run (order.rs): holds tells of every link and target in order and held_back is the first, a hold in the run or of no valid document is none, and an upstream the frame refuses on one link does not hide a pending one on the next; the upstreams refused are those the links resolve to now that a decision against stands about (denied, deprecated, retired), each once, and not one pending, approved, whose denial was revoked, or only pinned. The names of a document (doc.rs): what its links name and cannot be followed to, each with why, and the keys they write that are no document's, while a text that is no key gives neither. The use case (units_tests.rs): the pending are divided when asked, by the files their artifacts live in, with one ask of where they live, and nothing approved is nothing divided. The schema (output.rs): units is an answer of its own, with its fields, its counts of what could join units unseen among them, and the schema of stale holds none of them. The modules of the answers (split_modules.rs): the answer of units is a module of its own along the edges listed, with its warning, and the word of a hold, the word of why a link cannot be followed and the phrases of what could not be read are written in one place.
