What a content comes to (drift/tests.rs): one table of a document pinned with a hash against the same hash, another with no declaration, another joined by one, and a document not valid; of an artifact pinned there or absent against the same hash, another, another joined, nothing there and one not told; and of the comparison the same or changed, where only a thing there then and now is remade, one not told is not told whatever the comparison and one gone or come is that. A join that panics shows a declaration is never asked of two hashes that are the same, and a reader that asks none holds no kept meaning. The order and the moves (order_tests.rs): the decision that stands, the first of the readers and the approval of the declared status are one, the latest in the order of the records; a cut keeps a move exactly when it is before the decision in that order; of two moves of the same millisecond as an approval, of two names, a cut before the approval keeps the one of the smaller identity alone while both reach its pins, which only time decides; of two moves of one name at one time the larger identity is followed, and a chain at one time is followed to its end; every reader is a view of its own record. The snapshot and diff (snapshot.rs): a snapshot is read from the view of the approval that stands, the subject, each upstream as its pin and each artifact as its pin read now; an item of a document is the same, edited or not valid, an item of an artifact the same, edited, gone, not told, still absent or arrived, each with its hash now and whether it changed, and no one but SnapshotItem::compared makes what an item came to (a doc test that does not build). The approval that stands (doc tests of StandingApproval in ledger.rs): it is not kept past its ledger, and a reader of a content asks no reading, each beside a test that builds and differs in that one line. judge same as it reads now (same_meaning_tests.rs): it offers an edit declared before a move that stale tells as kept, and an edit of a pin of a document not valid now, and of one its link writes no more, which stale does not compare; the issue that makes it read the pins stale compares turns these. The one reading (approval_reads.rs, common/source.rs): read from the source of every crate, the token DecisionVerb is written only in the ledger, exactly five times (a re-export and the four arms of the view), and where a record is made, encoded and admitted, each as many times as listed; what it does not see (a re-export under another name, a macro, code after a module of tests cut inline) is on the test, for a review to read. Architecture (architecture.rs, arch-lint.toml): the rule of drift is run as arch-lint.toml declares it over fixtures in a directory of their own, and refuses a use of the ledger, of a module that reads the ledger, a path written in full in code and a use through crate, and leaves out a module of its ground and a file of tests; the rule of the ledger leaves alone a use of drift from a file of the ledger. The allowed modules are in the exclude of each rule and in DEC-97 and DEC-98, and this verification names the rules, so a change of the one makes the other stale. What every other reader of the pins and of the approval that stands says (freshness, readers, quorum, moves, stats, rekey, judge, the answers) is checked by the tests that were there, which assert the same of the same documents, now asked through the view of the approval that stands, `readers(..).at(..)`, the kinds of an item and `compared`. Two assertions changed, because the quorum or the reading of a document with no approval is no longer a question one can ask. The first test of quorum_tests.rs asserts, of a document with no approval, that it is `DeclaredNow::Pending`, where it asserted `Quorum::Met`; and the test of readers_tests.rs that asserted a quorum met of no readers is renamed `no_readers_are_read_by_nobody_and_no_approval_awaits_names`, and asserts that no readers are read by nobody and that a document with no approval is pending, with a rule or without. A test that read `ledger.reading(..)` for the names now reads `readers(..).at(..)`, and the one that read the quorum with them reads `StandingApproval::reading`.
