What a content comes to (drift/tests.rs): one table of a document pinned with a hash against the same hash, another with no declaration, another joined by one, and a document gone, which comes to its failure as it is; of an artifact pinned there or absent against the same hash, another, another joined, nothing there and one not told; and of the comparison the same or changed, where only a thing there then and now is remade, one not told is not told whatever the comparison and one gone or come is that. A join that panics shows a declaration is never asked of two hashes that are the same, and a reader that asks none holds no kept meaning. An edit tells the hash then and now: the same hash twice when it is the same, and the two hashes when it is edited or keeps its meaning. The order and the moves (order_tests.rs): the decision that stands, the first of the readers and the approval of the declared status are one, the latest in the order of the records; a cut keeps a move exactly when it is before the decision in that order; of two moves of the same millisecond as an approval, of two names, a cut before the approval keeps the one of the smaller identity alone while both reach its pins, which only time decides; of two moves of one name at one time the larger identity is followed, and a chain at one time is followed to its end; every reader is a view of its own record. The snapshot and diff (snapshot.rs): a snapshot is read from the view of the approval that stands, and a document with no approval, or one that was denied, has no snapshot; the subject, each upstream as its pin and each artifact as its pin read now; a snapshot keeps the revision its approval recorded and, of its four items (the subject, an upstream and two artifacts), three addresses, an artifact absent when pinned having none; an item of a document is the same, edited or not valid, an item of an artifact the same, edited, gone, not told, still absent or arrived, each with its hash now and whether it changed; an item of an artifact is held by the name it is read as exactly when it has a hash now, there as approved or edited, and by none when gone or not told, while an item of a document is held by no artifact name; and no one but SnapshotItem::compared makes what an item came to (a doc test that does not build). The approval that stands (doc tests of StandingApproval in ledger.rs): it is not kept past its ledger, and a reader of a content asks no reading, each beside a test that builds and differs in that one line.
