title: "A file is hashed by its link's comparison, and a changed comparison is told as such"
---
Lines ignores how much white space there is, blank lines and the declared comment lines, and not code or a space added or taken between two words; the pin keeps its comparison and a bytes pin keeps the form of an older record; a changed comparison is its own cause and no hash comparison; one file under two links is judged by each link's comparison. A link to artifacts compares bytes unless it declares otherwise, and lines keeps the ignore prefixes it writes; compare on a link into a book is refused, and so are an unknown kind, an ignore with no kind or with bytes, and a prefix that holds a space. Bytes is the hash of the content; a lone carriage return ends a line; a byte that is not UTF-8 counts as itself and is never the text that names it; a lines hash never meets a bytes hash or one with other prefixes; a reading has the hash of each comparison asked and of its bytes. A comparison is read from its words and writes them back, while another kind, an ignore with no compare or with bytes, and a prefix that is empty or holds white space or a control character are refused. A pin that writes the comparison bytes, a kind there is none of, a prefix that is no prefix, a key too many, a comparison that is no map, or prefixes out of order or written twice, is refused. A declaration of the same meaning holds under lines, told as a notice of a pin compared by lines; a declaration about one file under two links is made under the comparison that has an earlier hash, and with two such one naming no hash is refused, one naming an earlier hash takes that hash's comparison, and once one is declared the other needs no hash named.
