title: "Pending documents are chosen by one rule and approved upstream first, as stale ones are"
---
The pending rule takes a document never decided and one withdrawn back to pending, and not one approved, denied or invalid; the upstream of a named document is taken through an approved one in the chain; the stale rule leaves a broken approval out; an upstream in the run holds nothing back, and outside it holds back as pending or stale. Over the fake: every pending document is approved upstream first, a withdrawn one included; a reference takes only its pending closure; a named document approved or invalid is refused; a broken upstream and a stale one under --pending alone leave the named one out as stands_on; --pending with --stale is one run with one time; a refused one does not stop the rest; --until and --actor reach every record; nothing pending is an empty run; a document already approved on a pending upstream holds nothing back once the run approves that upstream; in one run of --stale and --pending, a stale document on a pending upstream is decided after it; a superseded pending document is approved with its replacement.
