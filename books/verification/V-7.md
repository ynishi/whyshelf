title: "A declaration is refused with every problem, and a sound one gives one grammar per book"
---
A sound declaration of three books makes a schema of three books, one keeping its name, its directory, its six fields and its three links; every problem of a declaration is returned at once; prefix and directory clashes and reserved names are refused; an empty declaration is a schema with no book, and slug books do not clash on a prefix; a name out of its grammar is told where it was written, and a directory that is no relative path, a key prefix out of its grammar, an enum with no word, a book, field or link declared twice, one name for a field and a link or for a link into a book and a link to artifacts, a link into an undeclared book and a replaces link into another book are each refused; a link to artifacts depends or informs, never replaces, and is kept apart from the links into books. A prefixed key is its prefix, a hyphen and a number of at most 19 digits with no leading zero, or a name, and any other text is refused; a slug follows the rules of an alias with no empty segment and is never a short id or a UUID, and a prefixed key is never taken for a short id. A file belongs to the book whose directory it is under and to none outside every directory, and a count admits the numbers it says; the sample declaration init --sample writes reads and is sound, with four books, the default space file and tests by the default preset. The declaration is read as the system reads its path, through a link inside the root or out of it; behind a broken link it is not there, and a directory in its place cannot be read (readers_tests.rs).
