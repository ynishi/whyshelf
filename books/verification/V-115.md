title: "The adapters list or refuse to list, the domain reads a listing in one place, and one assessment tells the gaps and exemptions of document and artifact covers"
---
In the crates: the file adapter reads a selection by the segment glob of a book's range and lists only the files a reading answers as there, and a listing it cannot make, cuts at a bound, or that takes a file a reading cannot tell or whose path is no name, is never all of them (file.rs, git.rs); the conformance test holds every adapter to its listing or to CannotList (conformance.rs); the dispatch answers CannotList for a space no adapter offers (dispatch.rs); the glob a book's range is read with takes what it matches, and the rule of a range refuses each pattern it does not admit (schema/scope_tests.rs); a cover with no selection, a table on a link into a book and a selection a space cannot read are problems of the declaration; a table on a link to artifacts whose space reads its selection is the cover of that selection, error unless it says warn; the selections asked of the adapters are those the declaration reads, two links with one selection in one space ask it once, and a link with a problem of its own asks nothing; parts are taken whole only through a link of the same book, to artifacts, that depends and compares content, and any other is refused with why (schema/cover_tests.rs); in the domain, a selection includes one text at least and holds no empty text, a listing gives its names only when it is the whole, one cut at a bound and one that cannot be made are told apart, and a selection not asked, like a command that asked nothing, is told as not asked, which is not a space that cannot list; a listing in parts is not the whole, its reason naming the first parts with why and counting the rest, and it gives its names and its parts, a part given twice once, only to a cover that takes its parts whole (listing.rs); declared_schema leaves no selection unasked, two links of one selection share one listing, check is not clean on an error gap, an incomplete listing or an empty selection, and the artifact exemption is framed with the listed names (artifact_cover_tests.rs, over the fake adapter beside it); an exemption takes the name of a part as that part, in the space of the link of parts, only when the cover reads the listing whole with its parts, and with a part in another space than that link, or a listing that is not whole, it is refused as one that cannot be told and writes nothing (app exempt_tests.rs); the gaps an assessment gives are the not_covered notices (freshness/cover_tests.rs); an artifact exemption is read needed, unneeded or cannot tell (exemption/tests.rs); and a document's exemption record keeps its bytes while an artifact's round-trips, and a subject that is a map with only a path, a space with no name, a key too many, or no text or map at all is refused (ledger/exempt_tests.rs). The file adapter lists no name outside a Git repository, and leaves out a file Git ignores (readers_tests.rs).
