Core: with no rule, any name's approval stands and is accepted; with a rule, an approval by a name of no group named is not used (pending, no decision), is a problem naming the record, the kind, the book it names and the groups, and is refused by accept, while one by a member stands and is accepted; a revocation by a name not admitted to revoke is refused, withdraws nothing and is a problem of its own beside the record's, and one by a member withdraws the record and its problem; a kind the rule neither names nor defaults is open; a record made before since is unruled; a member with an until is in the group only before it; a record is read with the book it names, with the book its document is in now when it names none, and with [decide] when it names a book not declared; a record with no key book keeps a form without it. Infrastructure: [[group]], [decide] and [book.decide] are read into the authority of the schema, with their times; a group with no member, a member whose from is not before its until, a rule naming a group not declared or no group, and rules of books with no [decide] are problems of the declaration; a time in neither form and a kind no rule has are failures to read the TOML.
