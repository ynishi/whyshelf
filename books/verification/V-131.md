title: "Two chains that share nothing are two units each upstream first, pending or stale, two chains whose artifacts live in one file are one unit, and what could join units unseen is counted and said"
---
On the binary and one small project of files (units.rs): of two chains of a requirement, the decision that serves it and the verification that checks that, which stand on files and tests apart, units answers no unit while all are pending, and with --pending two units, each upstream first, the requirement pending; once all six are approved and both requirements edited, it answers two units, exit 0 and no warning, each upstream first and stale, with the files of its own code and test, its test placed in the file before ::, nothing waited on and nothing unplaced. When the second decision's code is the first's file, or the second verification's test is another test of the first's file, the two chains are one unit, with both tests. When the first decision, approved, loses its title and a file of the requirements has a front matter that is no mapping, units answers the decision and its verification as one unit, counts one document whose links cannot be read and one file whose identity cannot be read in untold, says them in one warning on stderr, and exits 0; the clean answer above has no untold. When both decisions also serve a third requirement, approved and then deprecated with a successor, the decisions and verifications are one unit that waits on it once, refused and deprecated; when that requirement is not valid and never approved, units --pending answers the six as one unit that waits on it once, unfollowed and invalid, and counts it in untold. units --pending is one of the reading commands every answer and no write are checked of (common/mod.rs).
