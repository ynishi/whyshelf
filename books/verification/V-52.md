---
An approval that would repeat the standing one is left out as repeats with that approval named, and one on an edited document is written; --again writes; in a run of both, the edited one is written; a run of only repeats writes nothing; the same document named twice is written once; a later refusal still refuses the run; a standing approval fresh with absent names is left out, one fresh only by a declared same meaning is written, one whose expiry differs from the one asked for is written, a document rejected or withdrawn is written; an upstream whose key changed is written; the same approval twice over a pending upstream is written once; an absent artifact under a changed comparison is written; a past --until is refused, not left out; under approve --stale, on chains of three and four whose last is edited, the ones stale only through an upstream the run approved again are left out as repeats and hold back nothing, leaving nothing stale; the other verbs write as before. Another actor's approval of the same content is written, and then either reader's is left out as repeats naming it; with no name, a run that would only repeat fails and writes nothing, while a past --until and a reference that names nothing are refused before the name is asked. Under approve --stale by another actor, an expired approval is approved again, leaving nothing stale; on a chain of four whose last is edited, the two stale only through an upstream the run approved again are left out as second_reading and hold back nothing, leaving nothing stale; with no name, approve --stale fails and writes nothing. On the ledger: the same actor again repeats and another actor is a second reading; after a second reading either reader repeats the latest and a third actor reads again; content approved by one, other content by a second and the first content by a third leaves the third the only reader; an approval of other content, or with none standing, is new; a decision that is no approval ends the readers; a revoked approval reads nothing; a reader whose reading lapsed, or who asks another expiry than their own reading or the approval that stands has, renews, while one asking the expiry both have repeats; once the approval that stands lapsed, a reader whose own has not and another actor both renew it; another actor asking another expiry than the approval that stands, or none where it has one, renews it, and with its expiry reads it a second time; while a declared move reads a pin of the approval that stands, a new approval of the same pins renews it, by its reader or another actor, and read before the move it repeats; an expiry already past never repeats; read before a record, the ledger is as it was, records of one time taken by identity; a decision that is no approval is asked nothing; a named run leaves out a repeat unless again and writes a second reading, a chosen run leaves out both, and a new approval or a renewal is left out by neither.
