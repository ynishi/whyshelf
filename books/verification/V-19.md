title: "A document no covering link reaches is reported, and nothing else changes"
---
A covering link that reaches a document leaves no notice; one that does not gives a notice and an entry in check, whose schema names uncovered, doc, book, link and severity; a denied, retired or deprecated source does not count and such a target is not expected; a link that cannot be followed does not count, a link with no cover expects nothing, and two covering links into one book are each expected, each with its own notice; freshness is unchanged; a strength alone on a link to artifacts, with no selection, and a cover that is neither a truth value nor a strength are refused.
