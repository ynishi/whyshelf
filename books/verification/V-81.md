title: "A required text that is empty or blank, or a required list with no item, makes the document invalid; optional ones stay valid"
---
A required text written empty, as spaces, or as white space with a line break is told as empty or blank, naming the field; a required list written with no item too; an optional text written empty and an optional list with no item are valid; a required text that starts with a line break and holds words is valid; a required field left out is still missing, and a blank one comes before it; a new document with a blank required title is refused.
