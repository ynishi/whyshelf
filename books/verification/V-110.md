Core (stale_gate_tests): with nothing approved, and with one approval that holds beside a document still pending, the gate holds and passes (a_shelf_whose_approvals_hold_passes_and_a_pending_document_does_not_fail_it); an approval whose document changed does not hold and fails (an_approval_that_does_not_hold_fails); one that only expired fails once the clock passes its expiry (an_approval_that_only_expired_fails); one that pins a name its adapter cannot tell about now leaves no stale entry and the gate cannot tell, which fails, and holds again once the name can be told (an_approval_on_a_name_that_cannot_be_told_now_fails_while_it_stays_fresh). The command line maps the gate to the exit status of stale --exit-code, which the binary's tests check: a stale approval in stale_exit_code.rs (V-22), and a name that cannot be told in rust_items.rs (V-46, V-76).
