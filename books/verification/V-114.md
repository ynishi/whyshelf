On the binary (parallel.rs, keys_taken_on_two_branches_are_found_on_the_merge_result_and_renumbering_keeps_the_approvals): a sample project is copied into a second, and each adds a requirement, a decision that serves it and a verification that checks it, approved, so both take the same three keys. The second's files are copied into the first under other names, with its ledger records: the merge result of two branches, made without Git. check exits 1 and reports each of the six files as invalid for its key. Renumbering the copied group (the next number, REQ-3, DEC-3 and V-3, the file where new puts it, its key and the links of the group that mean it) makes check, fmt --check and stale --exit-code pass, and every document of the shelf, both groups included, is fresh in status.
