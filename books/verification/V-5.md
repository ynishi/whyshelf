title: "A declared edit keeps the approvals that pinned it, and a further edit does not"
---
The declaration reads back from its canonical form, one stored before spaces with a path alone reading as the space file; a declaration whose hash now is among its earlier ones, with no earlier hash, about a kind of thing this reader does not know, with its reason missing, blank or null, or with a key too many is refused; it covers the edit from one earlier hash, for the document's own approval and every downstream pin of that hash, and leaves an older pin from before an undeclared rewrite a cause; several earlier hashes need one named; a named hash covers itself and the pins already the same as it, and one no approval pinned or the hash now is refused; a pin of an approval that does not stand is not gathered; nothing is to be declared when every pin is at the hash now, nor for an edit declared once until it is edited again; a document not valid, a file no document links and a file not there are refused; a declared edit is a notice and not a cause; a further edit and a withdrawn declaration are causes again; a way of two declarations is named in order in the notice, and a declaration about another document or another path keeps no edit; declarations join the hashes they name both ways and along a chain, in the order of the way and by the shortest way whatever the order of the records, a hash no declaration reaches is not joined, a revoked declaration joins nothing, and two different declarations of one identity are set aside; a decision or a declaration can be withdrawn once, and a revocation, an identity no record has and one two records share cannot be withdrawn; nothing is written when one request of a run is refused.
