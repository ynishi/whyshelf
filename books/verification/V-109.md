title: "An approval and a declaration of the same meaning count their different names, kept apart from freshness, and a declaration that cannot meet them is a problem of it"
---
Core, the quorum (ledger quorum_tests): under a rule that asks for two names, one approval is short with its reader and need, the same name approving again with another expiry is still one name, and another name's approval of the same content meets it (an_approval_is_short_until_another_name_approves_the_same_content); a reader whose own latest reading lapsed counts no more, in either order of who approved last, and with every reading lapsed the reader of the approval that stands is told alone (a_reader_whose_own_latest_reading_lapsed_counts_no_more_whoever_approved_last); an approval no rule reads, lapsed or not, and one made before since need one name (an_approval_no_rule_reads_needs_one_name_lapsed_or_not). The same meaning: an edit is joined once two names declare its edge, a second declaration by one name counting once, and with no rule one name joins it (an_edit_is_joined_once_as_many_names_declare_it_as_its_rule_asks_for); names are counted per edge and never per way, and only the edges a pin that stale reads now reaches are told as short (the_names_are_counted_per_edge_not_per_way); an edge needs the least its declarations need, so a declaration after since beside one before it takes nothing back (an_edge_needs_the_least_its_declarations_need_so_a_declaration_added_takes_nothing_back); a name that declared an edge is refused declaring it again as already declared by it, beside an edge it has not declared only that one is left, and another name's declaration is its own, each through what judge same offers for the document (a_name_that_declared_an_edge_adds_nothing_by_declaring_it_again), and so is one that declared an artifact's edit under its old name and declares it under the name it was declared moved to (a_name_that_declared_an_artifact_edit_under_its_old_name_adds_nothing_under_the_new); an edge no pin that stale reads now meets any more is not told (an_edge_no_pin_that_stale_reads_now_meets_is_not_told); an artifact's edge declared under its old name by one name and under the name it moved to by another is one edge of two names, joined as freshness reads the pin through the move, and not told (an_artifact_edge_is_counted_over_every_name_of_the_pins_way_as_freshness_reads_it). Core, beside freshness (freshness quorum_tests): a fresh approval short of names keeps a fresh freshness, is shown awaiting, and is not taken by --stale, and a degraded one keeps its level with its quorum short (a_fresh_approval_short_of_names_is_shown_awaiting_and_a_degraded_one_keeps_its_level); an upstream that expired and is short makes what stands on it stale through UpstreamDeclared of Unseconded and holds it back as awaiting, before the expiry would let it through, while the same records with no rule leave it fresh and held back by nothing (an_upstream_short_of_names_stales_and_holds_back_what_stands_on_it_even_expired). Core, what settles an origin (settle.rs): an origin whose approval awaits other names is settled by an approval by another name and not by --stale (an_upstream_that_awaits_is_approved_by_another_name_and_not_by_stale). Infrastructure (books.rs): approvals are read from [decide] and [book.decide], one when not written; a rule whose names that may approve or declare a same meaning are fewer than its approvals, a [decide] that asks for fewer than a book, an approvals out of 1 to 10 are problems of the declaration, and one that is no number is a failure to read the TOML (approvals_are_read_and_a_rule_that_cannot_meet_them_is_a_problem_of_the_declaration); a [decide] whose since reads fewer records than a book that asks for two names is a problem, and one beside a book that asks for one name is not (decide_that_reads_fewer_records_than_a_ruled_book_is_a_problem_of_the_declaration).
