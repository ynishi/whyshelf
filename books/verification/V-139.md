---
The reader (docs.rs): a file with both sides of two documents is a conflict of one of the two identities, with the delimiter shared, with the marker first, or with a common ancestor of another identity between them; a byte order mark or a blank line before that marker is set aside; a conflict inside the front matter of one document, both sides of one identity, a side that is empty and a common ancestor of another identity keep the one identity; a side whose id does not read, and a side that starts with a mistyped opening, show none; an id in a later conflict of the body is not counted. A front matter broken otherwise with a conflict only in its body, a conflict with no end, a text that starts with a marker and opens no front matter, one whose side holds a thematic break, a side of a later conflict that begins with one, and a line of eight of the character are read as before; a front matter that reads stays read whatever its body holds, one that is not closed shows the id of any line, and a mistyped opening is said so whatever markers follow. The admission (admit_tests.rs): on a shelf whose only file is one whose identity cannot be read, a record that names no book is read with the rule of the whole shelf, where on a shelf that has the document it is read with the document's book.
