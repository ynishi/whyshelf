title: "In the crates, what an approval can pin of an artifact is there or absent, a name that cannot be told is told apart in one place, and a document that cannot be told is never read as gone"
---
In the crates (now_tests.rs): a document that is valid, invalid, gone or cannot be told keeps "cannot be told" apart from "gone" in what it fails with, its presence, the name it is read as, a lookup, the tag a retirement keeps and a snapshot, and only a valid or an invalid document has a file that carries it, an invalid one in the order of its files with the snapshot naming the first; a document that is not valid has changed from what was approved. What an adapter said is told as what an approval can pin or why it cannot: an approval pins what can be told and is refused what cannot, a name that cannot be told is no cause and a notice, a move is to a name that is there, a diff tells a name that cannot be told as changed, and two approvals that pinned the same content of a name stand beside each other while a change of what was there, present to present or between present and absent, sets them apart. A rekey target is one document on the new file whether it reads valid or invalid there, and one gone or that cannot be told is not the document planned (rekey_tests.rs).
