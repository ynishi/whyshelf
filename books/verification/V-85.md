The domain gives the next number after a chosen key, counted from the key chosen, refuses to choose again for a key asked for, stops after 64 keys naming the first and the last, and says when the grammar or the range has no next key. With files appearing at the first keys before each write, new with no key writes at the next free key with one identity and one asking of the adapters, new with a key asked for is refused and writes nothing, 64 keys each with a file give the refusal that says what to do and nothing is written, and a file whose front matter cannot be read does not stop new.
