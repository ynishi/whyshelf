title: "A file left in a merge conflict whose sides each show one identity holds one of them, and only those are told as documents it may hold"
---
A file left with both sides of a merge conflict whose sides each show one identity, and not the same one, holds the document of one of them. Its identity is one of those (ReadId::OneOf, which ReadId::of_sides alone makes, so it names two identities or more, each once), and the shelf keeps such a file in a list of its own, apart from the files whose identity cannot be read, which may hold any document. Whether a file shows an identity is asked in one place, and every reading below asks it there.

An identity no file carries cannot be told while a file may hold it: one a side of which shows it, or one whose identity cannot be read. The files that may hold it are one value (MayHold), those that show it apart from those that may hold any, carried as it is from what the identity comes to into every answer that says it cannot be told: the presence, the sentence of a refusal, the lookup, the rekey and the gate of impact. An identity no side shows, while no file whose identity cannot be read is there, is gone. The lookup by identifier and by short identifier finds an identity a side shows, with a record or without, and what it then comes to is the shelf's answer: get tells it as one that cannot be told, and an approval, an exemption or a frame refuses it. A retirement, which asks nothing of the state of its document, is made of it by its identifier, as of any document that cannot be told, keeping that it cannot be told and naming the book of the file; the document is then one the ledger knows, and is read as one with a record. A record that names no book is read with the one book every file that may hold its document is under, when a side shows the document; the filter of status by book takes such a document, when it has a record, in the book of any file that shows it, and a file that may hold any document is no ground for it. A file that carries an identity a side of another file shows, and that other file, are both invalid, each with the problem that names the other files, one for each identity, and the identity is then the invalid document of the file that carries it. reach counts such a file apart from the others it cannot follow (sided) when a side of it shows a document the walk did not reach, units when a side shows a document divided or stood on or named by one divided or of the chain, and the gate of impact does not pass while one is there at either end.

A document of a side that has no record is no row of status: check and health list the file as invalid with the identities its sides show (sides), and the gate stops on the file. A file one of whose sides shows no identity, or several, is one whose identity cannot be read, as before.
