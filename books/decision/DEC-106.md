title: "Whether a change makes an approval not hold is the comparison of the holding of each document at the two ends, by the causes it adds, yes, no or cannot tell, built in one place"
---
Whether the approval of one document holds, at one end, is Assessment::holding, the one entry for one document; it judges nothing of its own and reads, in this order, the standings (none listed is not_there, a fact and no judgement), the one judgement of a degraded approval that the set of degraded approvals is read from (with its level and its roots), the decision of a document that is no approval (pending, or a decision against it), and of a fresh one whether it awaits names; the names it stands on through a depends link that cannot be told are picked out in one place and read by it alone. So a holding is not there, pending, against, holds, cannot tell (it holds as far as can be told, and stands on such names), or does not hold. It is not what holds an approval back from being made. stale asks it once for each document, and makes its entries and its names that cannot be told from that answer alone; its gate is the fold of each document's holding (Gate::fold of the one mapping of a holding to a gate, Gate::from), which the use case makes as it asks each document and the report carries (StaleReport::held). impact reads the clock once, reads both ends at that time and assesses each, and for every document either lists gives its holding at each end, whether they differ, and what the change made of it (Made), built by one function: a cause is compared by the document that holds it and the cause itself, so another way to the same cause is no new one; a cause added that starts at an artifact the document holding it stood on, at the end before, by a name that could not be told is uncertain, and every other is certain; the name is matched through the moves declared of the pin (Cause::pin_names), so a move the change itself declares, which leaves the name before at the old one, still makes such a cause uncertain and never certain. A cause added is one the end before did not hold: a new edit of what already did not hold is a cause added too, so yes does not say the approval held before (its holding before says that). After, not holding with a certain cause, it is yes, with both kinds; else an uncertain cause makes it cannot tell from the end before (both, when the end after has names that cannot be told too); else, after, not holding or not told, with names that cannot be told, it is cannot tell from the end after, since such a name may hide a cause, or from both when the end before could not tell the same name; each name is listed once; else no. Only that function builds a Made, a MadeYes, a MadeCannotTell or an entry. Nothing new is made of what cannot be told: the adapters' and the assessment's answers are carried as they are.
