title: "Whether a change reaches a document is the union of six answers the rules of impact, the ledger, the shelf and the records already give, and what cannot be told is kept by direction, never read from the holdings"
---
Claim::of reads six answers for one identity, each from the rule that owns it, and decides none of them: the change adds a cause that makes the approval not hold (W1: impact of the change answers Made::Yes); it takes such a cause away (W2: the same impact with the two ends swapped answers Made::Yes, so putting a file back, approving again, a same meaning or a move that makes an approval fresh again reaches the document); what was decided about it differs (W3: Ledger::status_of of the two ends, read from the records and who may decide, with no pin); it is on the shelf at one end only (W4: Shelf::state_of is valid or invalid at one end and gone or unknown at the other); its content differs (W5: while it is valid at both ends, Doc::hash differs, the comparison stale makes of a document's own text, or what its links name differs, Doc::links_named, which the hash leaves out; or it is valid at one end and invalid at the other); a record about it is at one end only, by its identity, or not the same at both (W6: Record::about, each record's own word for what it is about; a revocation is about what the records it withdraws are about, at either end; an exemption added or withdrawn reaches its document so, though it moves neither W3 nor W1; a record rewritten under the same identity does too). A document such a record is about that impact has no row for (on the shelf at neither end, and no decision about it) is answered as well, by the same table, with that record its only witness: it was not on the shelf before, so it does not pass unless named. A link names a document by its identity while it resolves and by its text when it no longer does, so a document linking to one that is gone or no longer valid is edited though its own file is untouched (a retired document stays on the shelf, and a link to it still resolves). The causes of W1 and W2 are not carried: those of the change read forwards are in the impact row beside the verdict, and those it takes away are the causes of that row's holding before, so the gate says only that the change reaches the document. When none stands, what cannot be told is kept in a ClaimUntold, each way apart and as the rules answered it: Made::CannotTell of the change (adds) and of the change read backwards (removes, a Backward whose side is said in the change's words, the backward reading's before being the change's after); a document invalid at both ends (InvalidBoth: it has no hash to compare); a document on the shelf at neither end and unknown at one or both (UnknownAt, with each end's files that may hold it, as what it comes to there gives them: whether the change put it back or rewrote it cannot be told). Otherwise the change does not reach it. The holdings of the two ends, and whether they differ (impact's changed), are not read (F-70). The rows are impact's, every identity of either end's standings; both directions are built from that one set of identities, each row paired with its other (both_ways, beside impact, by the one function that builds a row), so neither direction can lack a row.
