---
One function of the domain, words::listed, joins names and words for a message, each marked or not and joined by and, or, commas or semicolons; the joiners of the messages it says are gone. A document is named by its key, or its identity when it has none (DocLabel); a link target that was resolved is named by its key. The writers of a key that a link names and two or more files write are named by their files, since the identity of one may not read (UnresolvedWhy::Ambiguous), and the one file that writes a key and carries no identity that reads is named as such (UnresolvedWhy::NoIdentity). An upstream a record pinned is named by the key it has now, with the key it was pinned under beside it when that is another, and said to be invalid, gone or not knowable when it is (FramePin::named, from Shelf::now_named). What a failed document is comes from one place, FailureTag::said_of and DocFailure::said_of, told of a subject and in full or short (short leaves out only the problems of an invalid document, never the files that could not be read), and the tag a retirement keeps and a snapshot reads of a document that is not valid is DocFailure::tag; a link that cannot be followed and a cycle (LabelledCycle, with the kind of cycle) have one Display each, and the command line calls them instead of saying them again. No message prints a value as debug output, except the control character a problem shows escaped on purpose. A key is kept in a declaration as written, a word or a table; slug, and the words of without_front_matter, are read by the one search of the words of a declaration, and a word an entry of a book does not take is a problem of the declaration naming the book, the entry and what it takes, told with the other problems of the declaration (check exits 1, and a command that needs a sound declaration exits 2), not a failure to read it; a key that is neither a word nor a table still fails the read of the declaration. map draws the word of a key from the domain, and its answer's kind of a key is that word.
