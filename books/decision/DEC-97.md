title: "What was decided against a document is read once from its record, and what an upstream is to what stands on it is one reading"
---
A decision against a document (denied, deprecated or retired: NotStanding) is read once, from the verb of its record, by Ledger::status_of, and every other place is handed that value (DeclaredStatus::Against) and carries it; which decisions go against a document is said on NotStanding alone, and a new one is a variant of it and of DecisionVerb, which every match over it must then read. The ledger reads, of the modules of the shelf, only those of what a document is (artifact, build, doc, graph and schema), which the arch-lint rule ledger-reads-only-its-ground keeps, so what reads the ledger is read by what is above it, and a new module is out of the ledger reach until the list that rule is written from names it.

A new approval is refused on a document decided against by frame_approve alone, from the declared status, so the ledger never reads freshness (DEC-5). A document decided against does not count, as the statement of DEC-65 reads it: of the declared status for an exemption, for the walk of what an exemption is read against and for the assessment of what is expected to be reached, and of the standing for Standing::stands, each by one conversion that moves the value and decides nothing. The refusal and the count are the same set, and they are two rules: on the day a deprecated document with successors may be stood on, the refusal changes in frame_approve and the count does not. A successor is a document that counts (DEC-16).

What an upstream is to an approval that stands on it is one reading of its standing, made once: no approval of it stands (it is pending, or it is superseded by successors, or it is refused: denied, retired, or deprecated with none), or an approval of it stands, awaiting the names its rule asks for or not, and passing its degradation (stale or broken, never an expiry) to what stands on it. The cause of an approval is that reading, with the one condition of a cycle added to a Via; its strength (DEC-15) and its event (DEC-71) are one variant of the cause each, so each group is one strength and one event. A new variant of NotStanding is stopped by the compiler where a status is read into a standing (stand_in_order) and where a standing is read as an upstream (UpstreamReading::of); a new group of an upstream that does not stand (a variant of Unapproved or of UpstreamStatus) is stopped at the strength and at the event (Cause::severity, Cause::located), which read a way of being refused (Refused) with a wildcard. A run holds back (DEC-51) by a match over that reading, and an upstream that holds nothing back is read only for whether the run left it out. A run does not say a refusal: whether an approval on an upstream is refused is the frame, and an upstream deprecated with successors holds nothing back. The exemption of a document decided against is unneeded or refused with which decision it was, and the words are the surface, one for each variant; a pending upstream is told as having no decision that stands about it, which is what the value says. The user-facing message of a refused successor (NotASuccessor) and the README keep their words, which say pending or approved for a document no decision went against.
