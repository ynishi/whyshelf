judge approve --stale takes no reference and a reason; with --pending it is one run that also takes pending documents (DEC-62). It approves again the approvals that are expired or stale and would hold once approved again; every other approval that does not hold is left out with a reason, broken (it is broken now), refused (the model would refuse the approval now, with the refusal's text) stands_on (a current depends link names a document that is broken, pending or stale and not taken by the run, or itself left out in the run for another reason than repeats or second_reading; a pending one is approved first with judge approve), repeats (stale only through an upstream the run approved again, with the run's --until the expiry it has, so an approval now would only repeat its actor's own reading, DEC-58), second_reading (an approval now would be of the content that stands by an actor who has not approved it, with the expiry of the approval that stands, such as one stale only through an upstream the run approved again, taken by another actor's run, which did not read it, DEC-58; an expired approval, or one the run's --until would make lapse at another time, is approved again by anyone's run), or not_admitted (the rules of who may decide do not admit the actor to approve in the document's book, DEC-88: the run approves the others and exits 0, and what stands on it is held back as left out, where judge approve naming the document would be refused, exit 1), and each is told on stderr. The candidates are taken upstream first (upstream_first) on one reading, so one pass sees every upstream decided or left out first, and each is decided on the ledger the earlier ones of the run left; everything decided is written together, and only a failed port or a broken invariant fails the run, writing nothing. Under --stale alone, pending documents are not touched. One --until applies to every approval of the run, an earlier expiry is not carried over, and one not after now is refused before anything is selected. A name with nothing behind it is pinned as absent and told, as by any approval; what is never approved again is an artifact pinned present and gone.
