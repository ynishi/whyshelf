Two names that cannot be told now are of one cause when their spaces are equal and the reasons their adapters gave are equal; the rule is one function of the domain, which both check and stale ask, and it gathers in the order each cause first appears. A cause holds back one pair of a document and a name, or many; the line for one is the line a name had before, and the line for many says the reason once, how many names and which documents, by key, the first three of each and how many more. The answers on stdout keep every name. A name with nothing there stays a line of its own.
