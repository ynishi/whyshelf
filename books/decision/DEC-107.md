units [--pending] is a command of its own, whose answer is a division and not a list; in the domain the choice is UnitsOf (Degraded, DegradedAndPending). The documents divided are the approvals that no longer hold, read from the one set of degraded approvals of the assessment, which what --stale takes, the entries of stale, the origins and the moves check lists are read from too; and with DegradedAndPending every pending document (order::pending).

The division is two steps with the one ask of the application between them. plan reads each document once: where it stands (the kind Standing::kind gives, carried, never written again), its footing (what its Depends links resolve to now and what its approval pinned, and the artifacts of both), the documents its Depends links name (those it cannot stand on included: one that is not valid, and each file that writes a key two or more files write), and every upstream that holds it back (the hold held_back tells of each link and each target, with every document divided in the run and none left out, held_back being the first of them). It reads the chain of those upstreams once too: each upstream outside the documents divided that holds one of them back, and each upstream outside that the same holds say holds one of the chain back in turn, until none is added, each with its footing, what its links name and its artifacts. It says which artifacts to ask about, those of the chain included (UnitsPlan::asks); the application asks the adapters where they live once, at the side after (DEC-99), an error of the whole ask being a failure of the port and a name an adapter cannot place being unplaced; and divide makes the units of the answers, reading a name the answers do not hold as unplaced.

Two documents divided, or of the chain, are joined when one stands on the other or names it; when an artifact of each lives in one file, whole or in part (Placement::files, so a file and two tests of one file alike); or when an artifact whose place cannot be told is the same name for both. A document of the chain is joined to what it holds back by the first of these, as what holds a document back is what its links resolve to, which its footing stands on; so two documents divided that the same chain holds back, directly or through what holds an upstream of it back, are one unit, and so is a document the chain shares a file or a document with. The chain is the work to be done before what it holds back can be approved. Only a pending upstream (without --pending) and one that awaits the approvals of other names can be outside, as a stale or broken one is divided and none is left out; an awaiting one is a fresh approval, which stands on nothing pending or awaiting, so it holds nothing back in turn and a chain grows through pending upstreams only. A chain stops at what holds nothing back: an approval that holds and is not awaiting, which is no key (as one, a root many documents stand on would make one unit of all; so the work of one unit can edit a file the artifact of a fresh document lives in that another unit stands on, and make that unit stale again), an upstream a new approval may not stand on (Ledger::may_stand_on), and a document divided. A document of the chain is no document of a unit: its artifacts and files are not listed in one, its places that cannot be told are listed in unplaced, no order passes through it, and waits_on gives only the upstreams that hold a document divided back. The file of a document and the declaration are no key: a document is the one document of its file, and a change of the declaration reaches every document.

The units are the connected parts of the joins: every document divided and every document of the chain is a node, the joins are made both ways and given to the strongly connected components of the graph, and a unit is the documents divided of a part (every document of the chain is reached from one, so no part is of the chain alone). The documents of a unit come in the order of upstream_first, given the documents divided of the unit alone, which reads what a document stands on and not what it only names: a document joined by a name alone comes in no order of that link, and the members of a cycle of links (a broken approval's, or a pending document's) by identity. The units come by the least identity of their documents, the files by path, the artifacts by space and name and the upstreams waited on by identity, with no map of hashes, so the same shelf gives the same answer. waits_on gives each upstream once with its hold (pending or awaiting), which is the same seen from every document it holds back. It writes nothing, and the exit is 0: a division is no verdict.

What could join units without the division seeing it is said, not left out: plan counts it once (Unjoined, carried as Units::untold, none when there is nothing), as the identities whose files are not valid, so that their links cannot be read, that are divided or that a document divided or of the chain stands on or names, and the files whose identity cannot be read, each of which may be any document. An identity not valid that nothing divided stands on or names joins no unit, and is not counted. The answer carries the two counts as untold, with the field names reach gives them, and the command line says them in one warning on stderr, in the words reach says its counts in; the exit stays 0.
