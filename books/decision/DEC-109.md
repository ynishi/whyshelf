Verdict holds a VerdictKind in a private field, so only the gate (within) makes one, and each variant is non_exhaustive, so another crate cannot build one from its public parts either. By what the change does to the document (Claim::of) and where it stands (Place::of): one the change does not reach is not_claimed, wherever it stands; one it reaches, or of which it cannot be told, that is named or reached by the walk is inside, with how (InsideBy) and what the change does (InsideClaim: the witnesses, or what cannot be told, kept as a value); one it reaches that was not on the shelf before, or that stands where the walk could not read, is unproven, with why and the witnesses; one it reaches outside is outside, with the witnesses; one of which it cannot be told that is not inside is undetermined, with where it stands (Away) and what cannot be told. Beside the verdicts the gate carries what cannot be read at either end (Unreadable): every file of the books whose identity cannot be read and every file of the ledger that is no record, before and after the change. Neither has an identity, so neither has a row or a verdict, and each may be a document or a record about one that the change touched, anywhere; so the gate does not pass while one is there, at either end, and does not guess which. A verdict passes when it is not_claimed or inside (Verdict::passes, the one place), and the gate passes when every verdict does and nothing is unreadable (Within::passes, the one fold); it is not the gate of stale (Gate::fold, DEC-26), which says whether approvals hold. The rule is stated once, on the page of the module within, which the command line, the README and the manual point to. impact --within <ref>... adds the gate to the answer of impact (within, in the JSON and its schema; null without --within): a verdict for every document impact answers, each tagged by its kind and carrying only its own fields, the named documents and their reach before the change, what cannot be read, and passes. Without --exit-code the exit is impact's; with it, 1 when the gate does not pass and 0 when it does, once the answer is printed; --exit-code without --within is a request error (exit 2), as is a reference that names no document; a failure of the reading is exit 3.
