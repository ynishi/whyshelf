whyshelf stats reads the ledger and the documents and writes nothing. One pure function of the two gives four measures, each defined once on it: approvals per document and book, those made after an earlier approval of the document that is not revoked but a second reading (DEC-58), which changed nothing freshness reads, and the revoked apart; the time from a document's identity, a UUID v7 that carries when it was made, to its first approval that is not revoked, per book, by nearest rank; runs, the decisions given one time by one actor, as every run of judge gives every decision of it the time of its reading (DEC-27); and origins, the pins held by an approval made again and by the latest earlier one that is not revoked, of the same comparison, whose hash or state differs; an artifact's pin is matched, and counted, by the name it is read as now through the declared moves, as freshness reads it, so a pin of the old name before a move and one of the new name after it are one pin, and when moves bring two pins of the earlier approval to one name each is compared, as freshness compares each, so the name is an origin when either differs from what the later approval saw there. A second reading is counted among the approvals and in its run. Whether an approval is a second reading is asked of the decisions and moves before it, with the revocations of the whole ledger, as every measure reads them, so a ledger already written may answer fewer approvals made again, and a later revocation may change what an earlier approval counts as. No measure reads a clock: since keeps only what was judged at or after it. The same ledger and documents give the same answer, order included.
