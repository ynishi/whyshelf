---
Who has read the content an approval stands on is one count, Readers::at(now): every name whose own latest approval of the same content (what an approval stands on as DEC-58 compares it, not the key of an upstream) has not lapsed, each once, and apart from them the names whose readings lapsed. ran_by is no reader (DEC-91). It is made once per approval by Ledger::reading, which walks the approvals that stand together once and gives both that count (ReadBy) and the quorum a rule asks for (DEC-89); Ledger::quorum keeps its form and reads the same walk, and keeps its own rule that when every reading lapsed the actor of the standing approval alone is what an approval waits on. A standing that is an approval carries the count (Standing::read_by), so a row of status, get and trace shows readers with the names counted and lapsed, in every project whether or not a rule asks for names. ReaderCount says nobody, one or several, and status --readers <word> (repeated as alternatives, combined with the other kinds as DEC-70 says) admits an approved document by it; a document no approval stands on has no readers and passes no --readers. stats and check are unchanged.
