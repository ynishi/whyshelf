A link to artifacts may declare cover = { strength, include, exclude }. The selection is text in the grammar of the link's space and is opaque to the domain; the adapter that lists the space reads it and says the names it takes. Listing is an optional ask of the adapter contract (DEC-84): NameList answers select, with no reading, and list; an adapter that does not list names CannotList, and the core gives its fixed answer. Of the built-in spaces only file lists, with PathGlob, the one grammar and matcher a book's range also uses (DEC-63), and only the names whose files are there as a reading would answer. A listing is All, Some (past a bound, or when a file it would take has a path that is no name or is one a reading cannot tell, such as one past the bound of bytes or a link out of the root: a name a document can write is never passed over in silence) or CannotList, and ListedNames is the one reader of it; a cover is declared only over a space that reads the selection, so a cover on a space that cannot list is a problem of the declaration, as are a cover with no selection on a link to artifacts and a table on a link into a book. One assessment, coverage::assess, called once by each assess of freshness on the same shelf and ledger, decides the gaps, the needed and the repeated exemptions and whether a listing is whole, for documents and artifacts alike; freshness tells its notices from it. A command that does not read artifact covers asks no listing. check is not clean on an error gap, on an error cover whose listing is not whole, or on one that selects nothing; a warn one is listed and told on stderr. whyshelf cover lists, per covering link, what is selected, reached, exempt and the gap. judge exempt --artifact records an exemption of one name from one covering link in the record of kind exempt, its subject a map of space and name, so a record of a document is the same bytes as before; it is read by the same rule as a document's (DEC-65), and unneeded once the name is not listed.
