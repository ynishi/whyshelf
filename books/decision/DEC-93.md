A link to artifacts may declare cover = { strength, include, exclude }. The selection is text in the grammar of the link's space and is opaque to the domain; the adapter that lists the space reads it and says the names it takes. Listing is an optional ask of the adapter contract (DEC-84): NameList answers select, with no reading, and list; an adapter that does not list names CannotList, and the core gives its fixed answer. Each adapter's page says whether it lists; file lists with PathGlob, the one grammar and matcher a book's range also uses (DEC-63), and only the names whose files are there as a reading would answer. A listing is All, Some (past a bound, or when a file it would take has a path that is no name or is one a reading cannot tell, such as one past the bound of bytes or a link out of the root: a name a document can write is never passed over in silence), Parted (every name of what the adapter could list whole, and each part it could not, a ListingPart: an artifact the adapter names in its own words, opaque to the domain as every name is, with why and the names found in it, or that it could not be read) or CannotList, and ListedNames is the one reader of it; a cover is declared only over a space that reads the selection, so a cover on a space that cannot list is a problem of the declaration, as are a cover with no selection on a link to artifacts and a table on a link into a book. A cover may declare parts = { whole = "<link>" }: the link is one of the same book, to artifacts, that depends on what it names and compares its content (bytes or lines, not presence), or the declaration has a problem, and a part is reached only through the link the cover names for parts, which depends on its content. Such a cover takes the names of a listing in parts by name and each part whole: a part is a gap, with the strength of the covering link, until a document that counts names it under that link, unless an exemption from the covering link stands of it, whatever was read of it, since a part read with no name found in it may still hold one that what keeps it from being listed whole makes, so whether it holds one cannot be told, and a person who holds it to hold none says so by an exemption with a reason; such an exemption stands only while no name is found in the part, so that a name found in it later is never hidden: the part is a gap again until a document names it under that link, and the exemption is told, with the names, as one that no longer stands (an exemption is not made of a part in which names are found); and a part in another space than that link's is a problem of the cover (PartOfAnotherSpace), which assesses nothing of the link, so that no gap is told and no exemption from it is told needed or not, and keeps check from being clean whatever its strength. A cover that declares no parts reads a listing in parts as one that is not whole. One assessment, coverage::assess, called once by each assess of freshness on the same shelf and ledger, decides the gaps, the needed and the repeated exemptions, whether a listing is whole and how each part stands, for documents and artifacts alike; freshness tells its notices from it. A command that does not read artifact covers asks no listing. check is not clean on an error gap, on an error cover whose listing is not assessed whole (whole, or in parts the cover takes whole), on one that selects nothing, or on a cover with a problem; a warn one is listed and told on stderr. whyshelf cover lists, per covering link, what is selected, reached, exempt and the gap, and each part with how it stands. judge exempt --artifact records an exemption of one name, or of one part by its name in the space the part is in, from one covering link in the record of kind exempt, its subject a map of space and name, so a record of a document is the same bytes as before; it is read by the same rule as a document's (DEC-65), and is unneeded once the name is not listed, or the part is no part of the listing, and once a document reaches the name through the covering link, or the part through the link of parts.
