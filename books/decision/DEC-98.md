title: "What a pinned content comes to now is decided once for each kind of content, from the approval that stands, and stale and diff read it; each place an approval stands on says it in its own causes"
---
What a pinned content comes to now is decided once for each kind of content, a document's and an artifact's (drift). Two hashes that differ are an edit unless a declaration joins them, and two that are the same are the same with no declaration asked (Edit). A document comes to that, or to why it is no valid document (DocDrift); an artifact comes to that under the comparison it was pinned with, or is gone, arrived, nothing then and now, or not told now (ArtifactChange), and when it is there then and now and its link declares another comparison it is remade, and no hashes are compared. The document itself and each upstream are one kind of content: the role is only the words each place says it in. freshness tells what each pin of the approval that stands came to in its own causes and notices (Cause and Notice, as before), and their strength is Cause::severity's.

Records are ordered by one key, the time and then the identity, for a decision and a declaration of a move alike, and the decision that stands is the latest in that order of those about a document that are not revoked (Ledger::standing_decision). What an approval is, is read from its record once, by the view of an approval (ApprovalView), the one match of the verb of a decision for what an approval stands on, lapses at and was made by; a decision of another verb goes against its document (NotStanding, DEC-97). An approval is a view tied to its ledger: nothing of it takes another ledger, the declared status is its owned copy (Approval: the decision, its frame and when it lapses), and every reader of a content (Readers), every approval stats sets beside an earlier one and every recorded approval asked about is such a view, as are the upstreams every approval pinned. Who read the approval that stands, whether enough did, and what its pins come to now are asked of it alone (StandingApproval, from Ledger::declared): a later reader of a content, or an approval before a cut, answers none of them. A view has no equality, and Readers is not compared.

A cut keeps the records before it in that order, decisions and declarations of moves alike. A move it keeps reaches the pins of an approval made at or before the move's time, by time alone, an approval of the same millisecond whatever the identities; of two moves of one name at one time the one of the larger identity is followed, as before, and a pin of a name as absent is read as written. One rule reads a pin through the moves, which the view reads (ApprovalView::read_pins).

ledger reads drift; drift does not read ledger; drift reads only artifact and doc of the shelf, which the arch-lint rule drift-reads-only-its-ground keeps, written from the side that is allowed as the rule of the ledger is, and the rule of the ledger (DEC-97) lets it read drift. The pins of the approval that stands are compared in the ledger, each once, and freshness reads what they came to.

diff compares an item, the content as it was approved, the way it was pinned, whether or not stale compares it now, and asks no declaration, so an edit declared to keep the meaning is an edit there (SnapshotItem::compared, from which what it came to, its hash now and whether it changed are read). Whether a pin is the same as another pin (DEC-58, stats) and which names a move may be declared of (DEC-59) are other questions.

judge same offers exactly the edits stale tells as changes, joining hashes along each pin's own moves; a pin stale does not compare now is refused with why, to mend or approve again; a second declaration by the same name is told per pin's way, with the declarations of the ledger it was framed from. It reads the same comparison of the pins of the approvals that stand, on the shelf they were compared on, and each pin is told apart once, with no arm left to a wildcard: a content compared now (the document itself or an upstream, each in its role, or an artifact), an artifact not compared as content (remade by another comparison, gone, arrived, still absent, not told now), or a pin stale does not compare now (every pin of an approval whose document is not valid, an upstream that is not a valid document, an artifact whose link no longer writes the name it is read as). A document, once it is valid itself, gathers its own pin and the pins of upstreams on it, those of an approval whose document is not valid among them; an artifact gathers the pins read as its name. Whether stale reads a pin now is decided in one place: it reads a content, an artifact gone or not told that pinned a hash, and an upstream that is not valid. The edits offered are the changes stale tells of those pins, of an artifact under one comparison: the one whose pins hold the hash named, the first in order when two do, else the only one with an edit, and with none named two are several earlier hashes. The earlier hash is the one named, else the one the document's own approval saw when that is an edit; another hash joins it only when the declarations along the moves of every pin that holds it join the two (for a document, those about the document); with neither, and several hashes, all are offered when one of them is joined by every other, and they are several earlier hashes otherwise, whatever their order. When nothing is offered, one refusal decides, in one order, over the pins that pinned a hash, the hash named or any: one that holds is not pinned when a hash was named (it is the hash now, or already means the same), else nothing to declare; else one not told now cannot be told; else one gone has nothing there; else one stale does not compare now is refused with the first such pin's reason, its comparison changed, its link no longer writes the name, or its document is not valid; else not pinned when a hash was named, and nothing to declare when none was. A changed comparison is a request to put right, as before (exit 2); a link that no longer writes the name and a document that is not valid are refused as what a person can set right (exit 1). A name that no valid document links, because its only linking document is not valid or its only link dropped the name, is refused as not linked (exit 2) before any pin is read, as names are resolved from valid documents as before. A second declaration leaves out a hash only when the same name declared it along the moves of every pin that holds it, with the declarations of the ledger the offer was framed from (Framed::not_declared_by). check lists as short only the declarations a pin that stale reads now meets (Ledger::same_meaning_short), in the order of before. That list still holds an edge met only by a pin gone, not told now, or of an upstream that is not valid, though judge same refuses those: stale still reads those pins, so the edge stays listed and comes back to judge same once the pin can be read again.
